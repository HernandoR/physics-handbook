\chapter{Elastic Waves}
\label{ch-30}
\zhtitle{弹性波}

\section[Waves in an Elastic Medium]{Longitudinal and Transverse Waves in an Elastic Medium}
\label{sec-30.1} 
\zhtitle{弹性介质中的纵波和横波}

\subsection{}\label{30.1.1} A body is said to be \redem{elastic} and its deformation, due to the application of external forces, is \redem{elastic deformation} if it completely disappears after the external forces are removed. According to \redem{Hooke's law} elastic deformation is directly proportional to the external effects that cause it, i.e. it has a linear dependence on these effects. At sufficiently small deformation, all bodies can he assumed, practically, to be elastic.
\zhpar{物体在外力作用下发生形变，若外力去除后形变完全消失，则称该物体为\redem{弹性体}，其形变称为\redem{弹性形变}。根据\redem{胡克定律}，弹性形变与引起它的外部作用成正比，即二者呈线性关系。在形变足够小时，各种物体实际上都可以视为弹性体。}

The elastic properties of bodies depend upon the nature of the thermal
motion of their molecules and their forces of interaction. For
example, a gaseous body changes its shape without hindrance to suit
the shape of the vessel it is contained in; gas has no \redem{elasticity of
shape}. But a gas has \redem{elasticity of volume}, i.e. the capacity to resist
changes in its volume. This property of a gas is due to the thermal
motion of its molecules and is manifested in the change in the
pressure $p$ of the gas when its volume $V$ is changed.
\zhpar{物体的弹性性质取决于其分子的热运动性质和分子间的相互作用力。例如，气体能够无阻碍地改变形状以适应容器的形状，因而气体不具有\redem{形状弹性}。但气体具有\redem{体积弹性}，即抵抗体积变化的能力。气体的这一性质源于分子的热运动，表现为气体体积 $V$ 改变时其压强 $p$ 随之变化。}

According to \redem{Hooke's law for volume deformation}, the change
$\dd p$ in the pressure of a gas for a small change $\dd V$ in its
volume is directly proportional to the relative volume defor­ mation,
called the volume strain. Thus
\zhpar{根据\redem{气体体积形变的胡克定律}，气体体积发生微小变化 $\dd V$ 时，其压强的变化量 $\dd p$ 与相对体积形变（称为体应变）成正比，可表示为下式：}
\begin{equation*}%
\dd p = -K \frac{\dd V}{V},
\end{equation*}
where $K$ is called the \redem{bulk modulus of elasticity} of the gas. For an ideal gas \ssect{8.4.1} the value of $K$ depends upon the kind of thermodynamic process of compression (or expansion) being carried out. At a very slow change in the volume of the gas, the process can be considered isothermal \ssect{8.3.7}, whereas at a rapid change it can be considered adiabatic \ssect{8.3.7}. In the former case $K= p$ and in the latter $K = \varkappa p$, where $\varkappa$ is the adiabatic exponent \ssect{9.5.11}.
\zhpar{式中 $K$ 称为气体的\redem{体积弹性模量}。对于\ssect{8.4.1}所述的理想气体，$K$ 的值取决于压缩（或膨胀）过程的类型：若气体体积变化非常缓慢，可视为等温过程\ssect{8.3.7}；若体积变化迅速，则可视为绝热过程\ssect{8.3.7}。前一种情形下 $K= p$，后一种情形下 $K = \varkappa p$，其中 $\varkappa$ 为绝热指数\ssect{9.5.11}。}

\subsection{}\label{30.1.2} The elasticity of a crystalline solid is due to the forces of mutual attraction and repulsion of its particles (ions, atoms or molecules) that make up the body and that have random thermal vibration about the points of its crystal lattice. The forces of interaction of the particle impede deformation of the crystal lattice that is associated with both the volume and shape of the body. Therefore, in addition to their bulk elasticity, solids also have elasticity of form, which is manifested by their resistance to shear deformation \ssect{40.3.9}.
\zhpar{晶体固体的弹性源于构成物体的粒子（离子、原子或分子）之间的相互吸引力和排斥力，这些粒子围绕晶格格点做无规则的热振动。粒子间的相互作用力阻碍与物体体积和形状有关的晶格形变。因此，固体除具有体积弹性外，还具有\redem{形状弹性}，表现为对\redem{切变形变}的抵抗\ssect{40.3.9}。}

The elasticity of liquids is also due to the forces of intermolecular interaction. But, owing to the fact that the average time $\tau$ that molecules of a liquid remain in a temporarily settled state \ssect{13.2.4} is very short, liquids, like gases, possess only bulk elasticity. They manifest elasticity of form only with respect to variable deformation of ultrahigh frequency with a period that is less than or of the order of $\tau$.
\zhpar{液体的弹性同样源于分子间的相互作用力。但由于液体分子在暂居状态\ssect{13.2.4}中停留的平均时间 $\tau$ 很短，液体与气体一样，只具有体积弹性。只有在周期小于或约等于 $\tau$ 的超高频变形中，液体才表现出形状弹性。}

\subsection{}\label{30.1.3} \redem{Elastic waves} are \redem{mechanical disturbances} (deformations) propagated in an elastic medium. Bodies that act on the medium and cause these disturbances are called sources of the waves. A member of
the audience in a theatre, for instance hears the speech and singing of the actors and the sounds of the musical instruments due to the variations in the pressure of the air, reaching the audience and caused by the sources of sound. 
\zhpar{\redem{弹性波}是在弹性介质中传播的\redem{机械扰动}（形变）。使介质发生扰动的物体称为波源。例如，剧场中的观众能够听到演员的台词、歌声和乐器声，是因为声源引起了空气压强的变化，这种变化传到观众处。}

\redem{Sonic}, or \redem{acoustic, waves} are elastic waves of low intensity, i.e. weak mechanical disturbances propagated in an elastic medium.
\zhpar{\redem{声波}（或\redem{声学波}）是强度较低的弹性波，即在弹性介质中传播的微弱机械扰动。}

Sonic waves, acting on the organs of hearing of people, are capable of producing auditory sensations if the frequency $\nu$ of their
corresponding vibrations lies in the range from 16 to \SI{2d4}{\hertz} (\redem{audible sound}). Elastic waves with a frequency $nu < \SI{16}{\hertz}$ are said to be \redem{infrasonic}, whereas those with a frequency $\nu > \SI{2d4}{\hertz}$ are \redem{ultrasonic} (elastic waves) with $\nu \gg \SI{d9}{\hertz}$ are often said to be \redem{hypersonic}.
\zhpar{作用于人耳听觉器官的声波，若其对应振动的频率 $\nu$ 处于从 16 到 \SI{2d4}{\hertz} 的范围内，即能引起听觉（\redem{可听声}）。频率 $nu < \SI{16}{\hertz}$ 的弹性波称为\redem{次声波}；频率 $\nu > \SI{2d4}{\hertz}$ 的弹性波为\redem{超声波}；而 $\nu \gg \SI{d9}{\hertz}$ 的弹性波通常称为\redem{特超声波}。}

\subsection{}\label{30.1.4} The propagation of elastic waves in a
medium does not involve the transport of matter.\sidenote{A certain transport of matter (mass transfer) may occur in the propagation in a medium of strong disturbances (for example, shock waves in explosions), when the vibration of the particles of the medium becomes nonlinear.} In a boundless medium, it consists in exciting forced vibrations of parts of the medium that are farther and farther away from the source of the waves. It is feasible here to digress from the discrete (molecular) structure of the medium, dealing with it as a \redem{continuous medium}, distributed continuously in space and possessing definite elastic properties. 
\zhpar{弹性波在介质中的传播并不伴随物质的迁移。在无限大的介质中，传播过程表现为逐步激发离波源越来越远的介质各部分做受迫振动。这里可以忽略介质的分立（分子）结构，将其视为\redem{连续介质}，即在空间中连续分布、并具有确定弹性性质的介质。}
\zhpar{注：在强扰动（例如爆炸时的冲击波）的传播过程中，若介质中质点的振动变为非线性，则可能发生一定的物质迁移（质量输运）。}

A \redem{particle} of such a medium, having forced vibration, is understood to be a small element of its volume whose size, however, is many times greater than the intermolecular distances so that it contains an enormous number of molecules. Practically, such a particle can be dealt with as a point because even in a gas the intermolecular distances are extremely small (of the order of \SI{d-8}{\meter} under standard conditions).
\zhpar{此种介质中做受迫振动的\redem{质点}，是指体积很小的一个单元，但其线度比分子间距大许多倍，因而包含极多的分子。实际上，即使在气体中，分子间距在标准条件下也只有 \SI{d-8}{\meter} 量级，因此这样的质点可以当作一个点来处理。}

\subsection{}\label{30.1.5} An elastic wave is said to be \redem{longitudinal} if the particles of the medium vibrate in the direction of wave propagation. Longitudinal waves are associated with bulk deformation of the elastic medium and can therefore propagate in any kind of medium: solid, liquid or gaseous. An example of such waves are the sonic waves in air.
\zhpar{若介质中的质点沿波的传播方向振动，则称该弹性波为\redem{纵波}。纵波与介质的体积形变相联系，因此可以在任何介质中传播，包括固体、液体和气体。空气中的声波就是纵波的例子。}

An elastic wave is said to be \redem{transverse} if the particles of the medium vibrate, remaining in planes perpendicular to the direction of wave propagation. Transverse waves are associated with shear deformation of the elastic medium, and, consequently, can be generated and propagated only in media having elasticity of shape, i.e. in solids. An example of transverse waves are
those propagated along the strings of musical instruments. 
\zhpar{若介质中的质点在与波的传播方向垂直的平面内振动，则称该弹性波为\redem{横波}。横波与介质的切变形变相联系，因而只能在具有形状弹性的介质中产生和传播，即只能在固体中传播。沿乐器琴弦传播的波就是横波的例子。}

Special properties are found in \redem{surface} waves, which propagate along the free surfaces of liquids (or the interface between two immiscible liquids), being a perturbance of the surface due to external effects (dropping of bodies into the liquid, the motion of ships, the wind, etc.). The forces of gravity and surface tension play a decisive role in the generation and propagation of these waves. In surface waves the particles of liquid have transverse and longitudinal vibrations simultaneously, describing elliptical or more complex paths.
\zhpar{\redem{表面波}具有特殊的性质，它沿液体的自由表面（或两种互不相溶液体的分界面）传播，是由外部作用（如物体落入液体、船只运动、风等）引起的表面扰动。重力和表面张力在表面波的产生和传播中起决定性作用。在表面波中，液体质点同时做横向和纵向振动，其运动轨迹为椭圆或更复杂的曲线。}

\subsection{}\label{30.1.6} A medium is said to be \redem{uniform} if its physical properties, essential in the problem being considered, do not change from point to point. A medium that is uniform with respect to certain physical properties may be non-uniform with respect to others. A mono-crystalline body, for example, is uniform with respect to its elastic properties and, at the same time, optically non-uniform for X-rays.
\zhpar{若介质中与所讨论问题有关的物理性质不随位置变化，则称该介质为\redem{均匀}介质。对某些物理性质均匀的介质，对其他性质可能是不均匀的。例如，单晶体在弹性性质上是均匀的，但对X射线而言在光学上是不均匀的。}

A medium is \redem{isotropic} if its physical properties that are essential in the problem being considered are the same in all directions. A medium that is isotropic with respect to certain physical properties may be anisotropic with respect to others. Cubic crystals, for instance, are optically isotropic, but are anisotropic with respect to other properties. Gases and liquids are isotropic with respect to any physical properties.
\zhpar{若介质中与所讨论问题有关的物理性质在各个方向上都相同，则称该介质为\redem{各向同性}介质。对某些物理性质各向同性的介质，对其他性质可能是各向异性的。例如，立方晶体在光学上是各向同性的，但对其他性质则是各向异性的。气体和液体对任何物理性质都是各向同性的。}

\subsection{}\label{30.1.7} A medium is said to be \redem{linear} if there is a directly proportional relation between the quantities characterizing the external effects being considered that act on the medium and the changes they lead to in the state of the medium. An elastic medium, for example, obeying Hooke's law \ssect{30.1.1} is linear with respect to its mechanical properties. A dielectric is a linear medium with respect to its electrical properties if its dielectric constant \ssect{14.2.4} is independent of the electric field strength. By analogy, a magnetic material is a linear medium with respect to its magnetic properties if its relative magnetic permeability \ssect{26.4.5} is independent of the magnetic induction of the field.
\zhpar{若作用于介质的外部作用与该作用引起的介质状态变化之间存在正比关系，则称该介质为\redem{线性}介质。例如，服从胡克定律\ssect{30.1.1}的弹性介质，在力学性质上是线性的。若电介质的介电常量\ssect{14.2.4}与电场强度无关，则该电介质在电学性质上是线性介质。类似地，若磁性材料的相对磁导率\ssect{26.4.5}与磁感应强度无关，则该材料在磁学性质上是线性介质。}



\section{Travelling Wave Equation}
\label{sec-30.2} 
\zhtitle{行波方程}


\subsection{}\label{30.2.1} \redem{Travelling waves} are ones which, in contrast to standing waves \ssect{30.5.4}, transport energy from one part of space to another.
\zhpar{与驻波\ssect{30.5.4}不同，\redem{行波}能把能量从空间的一个部分输送到另一个部分。}

The \redem{travelling wave equation} is the dependence on the coordinates and time of the scalar or vector quantities that characterize the vibration of the medium as the wave being considered is propagated through it. For waves in a solid medium, for instance, such a quantity may be the vector of displacement of the particles of the medium \ssect{30.1.4} from their equilibrium position or the three projections of the vector on the coordinate axes. Longitudinal waves in a gas or liquid are usually characterized by the surplus pressure of the vibrating medium, equal to the difference between its variable and equilibrium pressures.
\zhpar{\redem{行波方程}是描述波在介质中传播时，表征介质振动的标量或矢量物理量随坐标和时间变化的函数关系。例如，对于固体中的波，这一物理量可以是介质质点相对平衡位置的位移矢量\ssect{30.1.4}，或该矢量在各坐标轴上的三个分量。对于气体或液体中的纵波，通常用振动介质的\redem{附加压强}来表征，即介质的瞬时压强与平衡压强之差。}

A \redem{ray} is a curve whose tangent at each point coincides with the direction of propagation of a wave, i.e. with the direction of energy transport by the wave \ssect{30.3.5}. In a uniform medium \ssect{30.1.6} rays are straight lines.
\zhpar{\redem{射线}是这样一条曲线：其每一点的切线方向与波的传播方向一致，也就是与波的能量传递方向一致\ssect{30.3.5}。在均匀介质\ssect{30.1.6}中，射线是直线。}

\subsection{}\label{30.2.2} An elastic wave is said to be \redem{sinusoidal}, or \redem{harmonic}, if the corresponding vibration of particles of the medium is harmonic \ssect{28.1.3}. The frequency of this vibration is called the \redem{wave frequency}. Variations in pressure in a gaseous or liquid medium through which a sinusoidal wave propagates also take place according to a harmonic law at a frequency equal to the wave frequency. In a transverse sinusoidal wave, particles of the medium can simultaneously vibrate with harmonic motion at the wave frequency in two mutually perpendicular directions, each of which is perpendicular to the direction of wave propagation. Depending upon the kind of polarization of the resultant vibration \ssect{28.4.9}, distinction is made between the following \redem{types of polarization of transverse sinusoidal waves: elliptical, circular and linear (or plane)}.
\zhpar{若介质中相应的振动为简谐振动\ssect{28.1.3}，则该弹性波称为\redem{正弦波}或\redem{简谐波}，此振动的频率称为\redem{波的频率}。正弦波在气体或液体中传播时，压强的变化也按简谐规律进行，其频率等于波的频率。在横向正弦波中，介质质点可以在两个相互垂直、且都与传播方向垂直的方向上同时做频率等于波频率的简谐振动。根据合成振动的偏振情况\ssect{28.4.9}，横向正弦波的偏振类型可分为\redem{椭圆偏振、圆偏振和线偏振（或平面偏振）}。}

\subsection{}\label{30.2.3} A mechanical disturbance (deformation) propagates in an elastic medium at the finite velocity $v$. Therefore, disturbances, initiated by the wave source at the instant $t_{0}$ of time, reach an arbitrary point $M$ of the medium at the instant $t - t_{0}$. The greater the path $l$ that the wave travels from its source to point $M$, the greater the difference $t - t_{0} = l/v$. Consequently, the vibration at point $M$ lags in phase behind that at the source of the waves.
\zhpar{机械扰动（形变）在弹性介质中以有限速度 $v$ 传播。因此，波源在时刻 $t_{0}$ 引起的扰动，到达介质中任意点 $M$ 所经历的时间为 $t - t_{0}$。波从波源传到 $M$ 点的路程 $l$ 越长，差值 $t - t_{0} = l/v$ 就越大。因此，$M$ 点的振动在相位上落后于波源的振动。}

The \redem{wave surface}, or \redem{wavefront}, is the locus of all the points at which the phase of the vibration has the same value at the instant of time being considered. The difference $t - t_{0}$ is the same for all points in a wavefront. Through each point of a medium subject to wave motion one wavefront can be passed that corresponds to the value of the phase of the vibration at this point at the instant of time being considered. Many different values of the phase of a vibration correspond to a family of wavefronts. In a uniform isotropic medium \ssect{30.1.6} the wavefronts are orthogonal to the rays \ssect{30.2.1}.
\zhpar{\redem{波面}（又称\redem{波前}）是在所考虑时刻振动相位具有相同数值的所有点的轨迹。同一波面上各点的差值 $t - t_{0}$ 相同。介质中每一点都可以通过一个波面，该波面对应于此点在所考虑时刻的振动相位。振动相位的不同数值对应一族波面。在均匀各向同性介质\ssect{30.1.6}中，波面与射线正交\ssect{30.2.1}。}

\subsection{}\label{30.2.4} A wave is said to be \redem{plane} if its wavefronts constitute a system of parallel planes. In a plane wave, propagating along the $OX$ axis, all values of $s$, characterizing the vibratory motion of the medium, depend only upon the time $t$ and the coordinate $x$ of the point $M$ being considered in the medium. If there is no absorption of waves in the medium \ssect{30.3.7}, the vibration at point $M$ differs from that at the origin of coordinates $O$ only in that it is shifted in time by the amount $x/v$, where $v$ is the wave velocity. Hence, in a plane wave, propagating along the \redem{positive} direction of the $OX$ axis, $s$ is a function of the difference $(t - x/v)$ and the \redem{equation} of such a \redem{plane} wave is:
\zhpar{若波面构成一系列平行平面，则称该波为\redem{平面波}。在沿 $OX$ 轴传播的平面波中，描述介质振动的各物理量 $s$ 只与时间 $t$ 及介质中所考虑点 $M$ 的坐标 $x$ 有关。若介质中不存在波的吸收\ssect{30.3.7}，则 $M$ 点的振动与坐标原点 $O$ 处的振动只相差一个时间量 $x/v$，即在时间上推迟了 $x/v$，其中 $v$ 为波速。因此，沿 $OX$ 轴正方向传播的平面波中，$s$ 是时间差 $(t - x/v)$ 的函数。这种\redem{平面波}的\redem{方程}为：}
\begin{equation*}%
s = f \, \left( t - \frac{x}{v} \right).
\end{equation*}
Accordingly, the equation for a plane wave propagating in the opposite direction is
\zhpar{相应地，沿相反方向传播的平面波方程为}
\begin{equation*}%
s = f \, \left( t + \frac{x}{v} \right).
\end{equation*}

\subsection{}\label{30.2.5} The \redem{equation of a plane sinusoidal}, or simply \redem{sine, wave}, propagating in a non-absorbing medium along the positive direction of the $OX$ axis, is
\zhpar{在无吸收介质中沿 $OX$ 轴正方向传播的\redem{平面正弦波}，或简称\redem{正弦波}，其方程为}
\begin{align*}%
s & = A \sin \left[ \omega \left( t - \frac{x}{v} \right) + \varphi_{0} \right] \\
& =  A \sin \left(  \omega t - \frac{ \omega}{v}x  + \varphi_{0} \right) \qor \\
& =  A \sin \left(  \frac{2\pi}{T} t - \frac{2 \pi}{vT}x  + \varphi_{0} \right),
\end{align*}
where $A = \text{const.}$ is the amplitude of vibration and is called the \redem{wave amplitude}, $\omega = 2pi/T$ is the angular frequency of the wave, $T$ is the period of vibration, and $\varphi_{0}$ is the initial phase of the vibration (at the instant of time $t = 0$) in points of the coordinate plane $x = 0$. The quantity $\Phi = \omega t - \omega_{x}/v + \varphi_{0}$, equal to the phase of the vibration at an arbitrary point with the coordinate $x$, is called the \redem{phase of the plane wave} (see also \ssect{30.2.6}). 
\zhpar{式中 $A = \text{const.}$ 为振动的振幅，称为\redem{波的振幅}；$\omega = 2pi/T$ 为波的角频率，$T$ 为振动周期；$\varphi_{0}$ 为坐标平面 $x = 0$ 上各点振动的初相位（即 $t = 0$ 时刻的相位）。量 $\Phi = \omega t - \omega_{x}/v + \varphi_{0}$ 等于坐标为 $x$ 的任意点的振动相位，称为\redem{平面波的相位}（另见\ssect{30.2.6}）。}

\subsection{}\label{30.2.6} The distance $\lambda = vT$ that a sine wave travels during the time equal to the period of vibration is called the \redem{wavelength}. The wavelength is equal to the distance between two closest points in a medium in which the phase difference of the vibration equals $2\pi$.
\zhpar{正弦波在一个振动周期内传播的距离 $\lambda = vT$ 称为\redem{波长}。波长等于介质中这样两个相邻点之间的距离：这两点振动的相位差为 $2\pi$。}

Along with the wavelength, use is made of another characteris­ tic of a sine wave. It is the \redem{wavenumber}
\zhpar{除波长外，正弦波还有另一个特征量，即\redem{波数}：}
\begin{equation*}%
k =  \frac{2 \pi}{\lambda} = \frac{2 \pi}{vT} = \frac{\omega}{v}. 
\end{equation*}
Hence, the equation of a plane sine wave \ssect{30.2.5} can be written in the form
\zhpar{因此，平面正弦波的方程\ssect{30.2.5}也可写成如下形式：}
\begin{align*}%
s & = A \sin \left(  \omega t - \frac{2 \pi}{\lambda} x  + \varphi_{0} \right) \\
& = A \sin \left(  \omega t - k x  + \varphi_{0} \right).
\end{align*}
Accordingly, the phase of this plane wave is $\Phi = \omega t - kx + \varphi_{0}$. 
\zhpar{相应地，该平面波的相位为 $\Phi = \omega t - kx + \varphi_{0}$。}

\subsection{}\label{30.2.7} The \redem{wave vector} is the vector $\vb{k}$, equal in magnitude to the \redem{wavenumber} $k$ and having the direction along the ray \ssect{30.2.1} at the point $M$ being considered in the medium. The wave vector of a plane sine wave does not depend on the choice of point $M$, and the equation for such a wave can be written in the form
\zhpar{\redem{波矢}是这样一个矢量 $\vb{k}$：其大小等于\redem{波数} $k$，方向沿介质中所考虑点 $M$ 处的射线\ssect{30.2.1}。平面正弦波的波矢与所选的点 $M$ 无关，该波的方程可写成如下形式：}
\begin{equation*}%
s =  A \sin \left(  \omega t - \vb{k}\vb{r}  + \alpha \right),
\end{equation*}
where $\vb{r}$ is the radius vector of point $M$, and $\alpha$ is the initial phase of vibration at the origin of coordinates, i.e. at the point $\vb{r} = 0$.
\zhpar{式中 $\vb{r}$ 为点 $M$ 的位矢，$\alpha$ 为坐标原点（即 $\vb{r} = 0$ 处）的振动初相位。}

On the basis of the Euler formula \ssect{28.1.7}, the equation of a plane sine wave can be written in exponential form, which is convenient for differentiation:
\zhpar{根据欧拉公式\ssect{28.1.7}，平面正弦波的方程可以写成便于求导的指数形式：}
\begin{equation*}%
\tilde{s} =  A \exp  i \left(  \omega t - \vb{k}\vb{r}  + \delta \right),
\end{equation*}
where $\delta = \alpha - \pi/2$. Only the real part of the complex quantity $\tilde{s}$ is of physical meaning, i.e. the quantity $s = \Re{\tilde{s}}$. Therefore, in making use of $\tilde{s}$ to determine some characteristic of a wave, it is necessary to discard the imaginary part of the final complex expression.
\zhpar{式中 $\delta = \alpha - \pi/2$。只有复数 $\tilde{s}$ 的实部具有物理意义，即 $s = \Re{\tilde{s}}$。因此，在用 $\tilde{s}$ 确定波的某一特征量时，须在最终的复数表达式中舍去虚部。}

\subsection{}\label{30.2.8} A wave is said to be \redem{spherical} if its wavefronts are con­ centric spheres. The centre of these spheres is called the \redem{wave centre}. Such waves are generated in a uniform isotropic medium by a solitary point source. The \redem{equation of a diverging spherical wave} is of the form
\zhpar{若波面为同心球面，则称该波为\redem{球面波}，球面的中心称为\redem{波心}。这种波可由孤立的点波源在均匀各向同性介质中产生。\redem{发散球面波}的方程形式为}
\begin{equation*}%
\frac{1}{r} \, f \, \left( t - \frac{r}{v}\right),
\end{equation*}
where $r$ is the distance from the wave centre to the point $M$ being considered in the medium, and $v$ is the wave velocity.
\zhpar{式中 $r$ 为从波心到介质中所考虑点 $M$ 的距离，$v$ 为波速。}

In the case of a \redem{spherical sine wave}
\zhpar{对于\redem{球面正弦波}，有}
\begin{equation*}%
\frac{a_{0}}{r} \, \sin \left( \omega t - k \vb{r} + \alpha \right),
\end{equation*}
where $ a_{0}/ = A (r)$ is the wave amplitude \ssect{30.3.6}, $a_{0}$ is a physical quantity, numerically equal to the wave amplitude at unit distance from the wave centre, $\alpha$ is the initial phase of vibration at the wave centre, and $\Phi = \omega t  - kr + \alpha$ is the phase of the spherical wave.
\zhpar{式中 $ a_{0}/ = A (r)$ 为波的振幅\ssect{30.3.6}，$a_{0}$ 为一物理量，其数值等于距波心单位距离处的波振幅，$\alpha$ 为波心处振动的初相位，$\Phi = \omega t - kr + \alpha$ 为球面波的相位。}

In the exponential form, the equation of a spherical sine wave is
\zhpar{球面正弦波的指数形式为：}
\begin{equation*}%
s = \frac{a_{0}}{r} \exp i \left( \omega t - \vb{k}\vb{r} + \delta \right) = \frac{a_{0}}{r} \exp i \left( \omega t - kr + \delta \right),
\end{equation*}
where $\delta = \alpha - \pi/2$, $\vb{r}$ is the radius vector from the wave centre to the point $M$ being considered, and wave vector $\vb{k}$ at point $M$ is in the direction radially away from the wave centre.
\zhpar{式中 $\delta = \alpha - \pi/2$，$\vb{r}$ 为从波心到所考虑点 $M$ 的位矢，$M$ 点处的波矢 $\vb{k}$ 沿从波心出发的径向方向。}

Real sources of waves are always of finite size. But they can be dealt with as point sources and the waves they generate in a uniform isotropic medium as spherical ones if the distance $r$ from the source to the points being considered in the medium is considerably greater than the dimensions of the source. When $r$ is extremely great, any small portions of a wavefront can be regarded as being plane surfaces.
\zhpar{实际波源总是具有有限的尺寸，但若介质中所考虑点到波源的距离 $r$ 远大于波源的尺寸，则可将波源视为点源，并将其在均匀各向同性介质中产生的波视为球面波。当 $r$ 极大时，波面的任一小部分都可视为平面。}

\subsection{}\label{30.2.9} The propagation of waves in a uniform isotropic medium can be described by a partial differential equation, called the \redem{wave equation}. Thus
\zhpar{在均匀各向同性介质中，波的传播可用一个偏微分方程来描述，该方程称为\redem{波动方程}，即}
\begin{align*}%
\pdv[2]{s}{x} + \pdv[2]{s}{y} + \pdv[2]{s}{z} & = \frac{1}{v^{2}} \, \pdv[2]{s}{t}, \qor \\   
\Delta s & = \frac{1}{v^{2}} \, \pdv[2]{s}{t}.
\end{align*}
Here $s$ is a physical quantity that characterizes a disturbance propagating in the medium at the velocity $v$, 
\zhpar{式中 $s$ 为表征在介质中以速度 $v$ 传播的扰动的物理量，}
\begin{equation*}%
\Delta = \pdv[2]{x} + \pdv[2]{y} + \pdv[2]{z},
\end{equation*}
is the Laplace operator, or Laplacian. This equation is satisfied, among others, by a plane wave \ssect{30.2.7} and a diverging spherical wave \ssect{30.2.8}.
\zhpar{该算符称为拉普拉斯算符（或拉普拉斯算子）。该方程对平面波\ssect{30.2.7}和发散球面波\ssect{30.2.8}等均成立。}

Function $s$, characterizing a \redem{sine wave} in a uniform isotropic medium, satisfies two partial differential equations at the same time:
\zhpar{在均匀各向同性介质中，表征\redem{正弦波}的函数 $s$ 同时满足两个偏微分方程：}
\begin{equation*}%
\Delta s = -k^{2}s,
\end{equation*}
where $k$ is the wavenumber \ssect{30.2.6}, and
\zhpar{式中 $k$ 为波数\ssect{30.2.6}，且}
\begin{equation*}%
\pdv[2]{s}{t} = -k^{2}s,
\end{equation*}
where co is the angular frequency of the wave.
\zhpar{式中 $\omega$ 为波的角频率。}

\subsection{}\label{30.2.10} The velocity $v$ of propagation of a \redem{sine wave} is called the \redem{phase velocity}. It is equal to the velocity of displacement in space of the points in a surface that corresponds to any fixed value of the phase of a sine wave. For example, in the case of a plane sine wave \ssect{30.2.6} it follows from the condition $\omega t - kx + \varphi_{0} = \text{const.}$ that $\dv*{x}{t} = \omega/k = v$. In the same way, in the case of a spherical sine wave \ssect{30.2.8}, it follows from the condition $\omega t - kr + \alpha = \text{const.}$ that $\dv*{r}{t} = \omega / k = v$.
\zhpar{正弦波的传播速度 $v$ 称为\redem{相速度}。它等于与正弦波某一固定相位值对应的波面上各点在空间中的移动速度。例如，对于平面正弦波\ssect{30.2.6}，由条件 $\omega t - kx + \varphi_{0} = \text{const.}$ 可得 $\dv*{x}{t} = \omega/k = v$。同理，对于球面正弦波\ssect{30.2.8}，由条件 $\omega t - kr + \alpha = \text{const.}$ 可得 $\dv*{r}{t} = \omega / k = v$。}

\section{Phase Velocity and Energy of Elastic Waves}
\label{sec-30.3} 
\zhtitle{弹性波的相速度与能量}


\subsection{}\label{30.3.1} The \redem{phase velocity of sonic waves (velocity of sound) in a liquid or gas} is
\zhpar{\redem{液体或气体中声波（声速）的相速度}为}
\begin{equation*}%
v = \sqrt{\frac{K}{\rho}},
\end{equation*}
where $\rho$ is the density of the undisturbed medium, and $K$ is the bulk modulus of elasticity of the medium \ssect{30.1.1}. The frequency of audible sonic waves $\nu > \SI{16}{\hertz}$, and the process of deforming the medium can be assumed to be adiabatic \ssect{8.3.7}, i.e. $K = -V (\dv*{p}{V})_{ad}$. The relation between the pressure $p$ and volume $V$ for an ideal gas \ssect{8.4.1} in an adiabatic process is: $pV^{\varkappa} = \text{const.}$, where $\varkappa$ is the adiabatic exponent \ssect{9.5.11}, so that $K = \varkappa p$. Therefore, the \redem{velocity of sound in an ideal gas} is equal to
\zhpar{式中 $\rho$ 为未受扰动介质的密度，$K$ 为介质的体积弹性模量\ssect{30.1.1}。可听声波的频率 $\nu > \SI{16}{\hertz}$，且介质的形变过程可视为绝热过程\ssect{8.3.7}，即 $K = -V (\dv*{p}{V})_{ad}$。对于理想气体\ssect{8.4.1}，绝热过程中压强 $p$ 与体积 $V$ 的关系为 $pV^{\varkappa} = \text{const.}$，其中 $\varkappa$ 为绝热指数\ssect{9.5.11}，因而 $K = \varkappa p$。因此，\redem{理想气体中的声速}为}
\begin{equation*}%
v = \sqrt{\frac{\varkappa p}{\rho}} = \sqrt{\frac{\varkappa RT}{\mu}},
\end{equation*}
where $\mu$, is the molar mass of the gas, $T$ is its absolute temperature, and $R$ is the universal gas constant.
\zhpar{式中 $\mu$ 为气体的摩尔质量，$T$ 为其热力学温度，$R$ 为摩尔气体常量。}

\subsection{}\label{30.3.2} The \redem{phase velocity of transverse elastic waves} in a uniform isotropic solid medium is
\zhpar{在均匀各向同性的固体介质中，\redem{横向弹性波的相速度}为}
\begin{equation*}%
v = \sqrt{\frac{G}{\rho}},
\end{equation*}
where $G$ is the shear modulus of the medium \ssect{40.3.9} and $\rho$ is its density.
\zhpar{式中 $G$ 为介质的切变模量\ssect{40.3.9}，$\rho$ 为其密度。}

The propagation of longitudinal waves in a \redem{thin long rod} is associated with its longitudinal tension and compression. Hence, the phase velocity of such waves is
\zhpar{纵波在\redem{细长棒}中的传播与棒的纵向拉伸和压缩相联系。因此，此类波的相速度为}
\begin{equation*}%
v = \sqrt{\frac{E}{\rho}},
\end{equation*}
where $E$ is Young's modulus \ssect{40.3.6} for the rod material.
\zhpar{式中 $E$ 为棒材料的杨氏模量\ssect{40.3.6}。}

The \redem{velocity of propagation of transverse waves along a stretched string (a thin flexible cord)} is
\zhpar{沿\redem{张紧弦}（细长柔软的绳索）传播的横波速度为}
\begin{equation*}%
v = \sqrt{\frac{F}{\rho S}},
\end{equation*}
where $F$ is the stretching force (tension) in the string, and $\rho$ and $S$ are the density of the string material and the cross-sectional area of the string.
\zhpar{式中 $F$ 为弦中的张力（拉力），$\rho$ 和 $S$ 分别为弦材料的密度和弦的横截面积。}

\subsection{}\label{30.3.3} An elastic medium in which mechanical waves propagate has kinetic energy of vibratory motion of its particles and potential energy due to deformation \ssect{30.1.1}. If $\vb{v}_{1}$ the velocity of particles of the medium, then the \redem{volume kinetic energy density of the medium} is 
\zhpar{传播机械波的弹性介质，既具有其质点振动的动能，又具有因形变而产生的势能\ssect{30.1.1}。若 $\vb{v}_{1}$ 为介质质点的速度，则介质的\redem{体积动能密度}为}
\begin{equation*}%
E^{d}_{k} = \dv{E_{k}}{V} = \frac{1}{2} \rho v_{1}^{2},
\end{equation*}
where $\rho$ is the density of the medium, and $\dd E_{k}$ is the kinetic energy of all the particles in the small volume $\dd V$ of the medium. This volume has been selected so that throughout it the velocity $\vb{v}_{1}$ is the same.
\zhpar{式中 $\rho$ 为介质的密度，$\dd E_{k}$ 为介质中体积为 $\dd V$ 的小体积元内所有质点的动能。选取这一体积元时，应使其内各点的速度 $\vb{v}_{1}$ 相同。}

The \redem{volume potential energy density of an elastically deformed medium} is
\zhpar{弹性形变介质的\redem{体积势能密度}为}
\begin{equation*}%
E^{d}_{p} = \dv{E_{p}}{V} = \frac{1}{2} \rho v^{2} \varepsilon^{2},
\end{equation*}
where $\dd E_{p}$ is the potential energy of a uniformly deformed
small portion of the medium of volume $\dd V$, $v$ is the phase velocity in the medium, and $\varepsilon$ is the relative deformation. 
\zhpar{式中 $\dd E_{p}$ 为体积为 $\dd V$ 的均匀形变小部分介质的势能，$v$ 为介质中的相速度，$\varepsilon$ 为相对形变。}

The \redem{volume energy density of elastic waves} is understood to be the volume density $E^{d}$ of the mechanical energy of a medium due to the propagation of these waves. Thus
\zhpar{弹性波的\redem{体积能量密度}是指介质中由于这些波的传播而具有的机械能的体积密度 $E^{d}$。即}
\begin{equation*}%
E^{d} = E^{d}_{k} + E^{d}_{p} =  \frac{1}{2} \rho ( v_{1}^{2} + v^{2} \varepsilon^{2}).
\end{equation*}

\subsection{}\label{30.3.4} If a longitudinal plane travelling wave \ssect{30.2.4} propagates in a medium, then $v_{1} = \pdv*{s}{t}$, where $s$ is the displacement of the particles, and $\varepsilon = \pdv*{s}{x} = - v_{1}/v$, so that
\zhpar{若纵向平面行波\ssect{30.2.4}在介质中传播，则 $v_{1} = \pdv*{s}{t}$，其中 $s$ 为质点的位移，且 $\varepsilon = \pdv*{s}{x} = - v_{1}/v$，因此}
\begin{equation*}%
E^{d}_{p} = E^{d}_{k} \qand E^{d} = \rho v_{1}^{2} = \rho \left( \pdv{s}{t}\right)^{2}.
\end{equation*}
At each point of the medium subject to wave motion, $E^{d}_{k}$ and
$E^{d}_{p} $ are \redem{alike} functions of time. Consequently, $E^{d}$ also varies with time. This principle is valid for any travelling waves in an elastic medium, regardless of the shape of their wavefronts and the type of deformation of the medium. It follows from the law of conservation of energy as applied to the process of the propagation of vibrations in an elastic medium. 
\zhpar{在波动所及的介质的每一点，$E^{d}_{k}$ 与 $E^{d}_{p}$ 是\redem{同步}变化的函数，因而 $E^{d}$ 也随时间变化。这一结论对弹性介质中的任何行波都成立，与波面的形状和介质的形变类型无关。它是能量守恒定律应用于弹性介质中振动传播过程的结果。}

To set regions of the medium more and more distant from the wave source into vibratory motion it is necessary to expend energy, which is supplied to the medium by the source. Consequently, the propagation of elastic waves is inseparably associated with the transport of energy from certain parts of the medium to others. This is precisely why the volume energy density $E^{d}$ of the waves depends both on the coordinates and on the time as well.
\zhpar{要使离波源越来越远的介质区域开始振动，需要消耗能量，这些能量由波源提供给介质。因此，弹性波的传播必然伴随着能量从介质的某些部分向另一些部分的输运。这正是波的体积能量密度 $E^{d}$ 同时依赖于坐标和时间的原因。}

For a plane travelling sine wave in a non-absorbing medium \ssect{30.2.5}
\zhpar{对于无吸收介质中的平面行波正弦波\ssect{30.2.5}，}
\begin{align*}%
E^{d} & = \rho A^{2} \omega^{2} \cos^{2} (\omega t - kx + \varphi_{0}) \\
& = \frac{1}{2} \rho A^{2}\omega^{2} [1 + \cos 2(\omega t - kx + \varphi_{0})],
\end{align*}
where $A = \text{const.}$ is the wave amplitude.
\zhpar{式中 $A = \text{const.}$ 为波的振幅。}

In the case of a diverging spherical sine wave in a non-absorbing medium \ssect{30.2.8}
\zhpar{对于无吸收介质中的发散球面正弦波\ssect{30.2.8}，}
\begin{equation*}%
E^{d} = \rho A^{2}\omega^{2}\cos^{2} (\omega t - kr + \alpha),
\end{equation*}
where $A = a_{0}/r$ is the amplitude of the wave.
\zhpar{式中 $A = a_{0}/r$ 为波的振幅。}

The average volume energy density over one period is
\zhpar{一个周期内的平均体积能量密度为}
\begin{equation*}%
E^{d} = \frac{1}{2} \rho A^{2} \omega^{2}.
\end{equation*}
%Fig. 30.1 

\subsection{}\label{30.3.5} The \redem{velocity of energy transfer
by a wave} is equal to the velocity in space of a surface corresponding to the maximum value of the volume density $E^{d}$ of the wave's energy. For sine waves this velocity is equal to the phase velocity $v$.
\zhpar{波的\redem{能量传递速度}等于与波的体积能量密度 $E^{d}$ 的最大值相对应的面在空间中的移动速度。对于正弦波，该速度等于相速度 $v$。}

The \redem{energy flux} $\dd \Phi_{E}$ through the small surface $\dd S$ is the ratio of the energy $\dd E$ transferred through this surface in a short lime interval to the magnitude of this interval $\dd t$. Thus
\zhpar{在短时间间隔 $\dd t$ 内通过某一小面积 $\dd S$ 所传递的能量 $\dd E$，与该时间间隔之比，称为通过该面积的\redem{能流} $\dd \Phi_{E}$。即}
\begin{equation*}%
\dd \Phi_{E} = \dv{E}{t}.
\end{equation*}
\begin{marginfigure}%[-1.5cm]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.8\textwidth]{figs/ch-30/fig-30-01.pdf}
\sidecaption{Velocity vector of energy transfer by the wave.\zhcap{波的能量传递速度矢量。}\label{fig-30-01}}
\end{marginfigure}
If $v$ is the velocity vector of energy transfer by the wave (\figr{fig-30-01}), then
\zhpar{若 $v$ 为波的能量传递速度矢量（见\figr{fig-30-01}），则}
\begin{align*}%
\dd E & = E^{d} v \, \dd t \, \dd S \, \cos \alpha = E^{d}(\vb{v}\dd \vb{S}) \, \dd t \qand \\
\dd \Phi_{E} & =  E^{d}\, (\vb{v}\dd \vb{S})= (\vb{U}\,\dd \vb{S}),
\end{align*}
where $E^{d}$ is the volume energy density of the wave, $\dd \vb{S} = \vu{n} \dd S$  is the vector of surface $\dd S$, $\vu{n}$ is a unit vector of the normal to the surface, and $\alpha$ is the angle between $\vb{v}$ and $\dd \vb{S}$.
\zhpar{式中 $E^{d}$ 为波的体积能量密度，$\dd \vb{S} = \vu{n} \dd S$ 为面元 $\dd S$ 的矢量，$\vu{n}$ 为该面的法向单位矢量，$\alpha$ 为 $\vb{v}$ 与 $\dd \vb{S}$ 之间的夹角。}

The vector $\vb{U} = E^{d}\vb{v}$, having the direction of energy transfer by the wave, is called the \redem{$U_{mov}$ vector (energy flux density vector of the wave)}. In magnitude it equals the ratio of the energy flux $\dd \Phi_{E}$ through the small surface $\dd S$ to the area $\dd S_{\perp} = \dd S \cos \alpha$ of the projection of this surface on a plane perpendicular to the direction of energy transfer: $U = \dv*{\Phi_{E}}{S_{\perp}}$.
\zhpar{矢量 $\vb{U} = E^{d}\vb{v}$ 的方向与波的能量传递方向一致，称为\redem{$U_{mov}$ 矢量（波的能流密度矢量）}。其大小等于通过小面积 $\dd S$ 的能流 $\dd \Phi_{E}$ 与该面在垂直于能量传递方向的平面上的投影面积 $\dd S_{\perp} = \dd S \cos \alpha$ 之比，即 $U = \dv*{\Phi_{E}}{S_{\perp}}$。}

\subsection{}\label{30.3.6} The \redem{wave intensity} $I$ is the magnitude of the average value of the $U_{mov}$ vector. Wave intensity is numerically equal to the energy transferred by the wave in unit time through unit area of a surface perpendicular to the direction of wave propagation. The intensity of a travelling sine wave is proportional to the square of its amplitude. For plane and spherical sine waves
\zhpar{\redem{波的强度} $I$ 是 $U_{mov}$ 矢量平均值的大小。波的强度在数值上等于单位时间内通过垂直于波传播方向的单位面积的能量。行波正弦波的强度与其振幅的平方成正比。对于平面正弦波和球面正弦波，}
\begin{equation*}%
I = \abs{\expval{\vb{U}}} = v \expval{E^{d}} = \frac{1}{2} \rho v \omega^{2} A^{2}.
\end{equation*}
When a spherical wave propagates in a non-absorbing medium, the same amount of energy is transferred in unit time through any spherical surface of radius r with its centre at the wave centre. This energy is equal to that expended during the same time by the wave source: $4\pi r^{2}I = \text{const.}$ Hence, the intensity and amplitude of a spherical wave decrease with an increase in the distance from the wave centre according to the relations
\zhpar{球面波在无吸收介质中传播时，单位时间内通过以波心为球心、半径为 $r$ 的任一球面的能量都相同，并且等于同一时间内波源所消耗的能量：$4\pi r^{2}I = \text{const.}$。因此，球面波的强度和振幅随距波心距离的增大而减小，其关系为}
\begin{equation*}%
I(r) = \frac{i_{0}}{r^{2}} \qand A(r) = \frac{a_{0}}{r},
\end{equation*}
where $i_{0}$ and $a_{0}$ are physical quantities numerically equal to the intensity and amplitude of the wave at the distance $r = \SI{1}{\meter}$ from the wave centre.
\zhpar{式中 $i_{0}$ 和 $a_{0}$ 为物理量，其数值分别等于距波心 $r = \SI{1}{\meter}$ 处波的强度和振幅。}

It can be shown in the same way that in the case of a plane sine wave in a non-absorbing medium, the amplitude $A$ of the wave is independent of the coordinates.
\zhpar{同理可证，在无吸收介质中，平面正弦波的振幅 $A$ 与坐标无关。}

\subsection{}\label{30.3.7} The conversion of wave energy into other forms of energy, taking place in the propagation of waves through a medium, is called wave absorption. In a uniform medium the absorption of elastic waves is due chiefly to the processes of internal friction, or viscosity \ssect{10.8.4}, and heat conduction \ssect{10.8.5}. The amplitude $A$ and intensity $I$ of a plane wave, propagating in an absorbing medium along the positive direction of axis $OX$, varies according to the exponential law
\zhpar{波在介质中传播时，其能量转化为其他形式能量的过程称为\redem{波的吸收}。在均匀介质中，弹性波的吸收主要由内摩擦（即黏性）\ssect{10.8.4}和热传导\ssect{10.8.5}过程引起。沿 $OX$ 轴正方向在吸收介质中传播的平面波，其振幅 $A$ 和强度 $I$ 按指数规律变化：}
\begin{equation*}%
A(x)= A_{0} \exp(- \alpha x) \qand I (x)= I_{0}\exp(-2 \alpha x).
\end{equation*}
Here $A_{0}$ and $I_{0}$ are the amplitude and intensity of the wave at the points $x = 0$, and $\alpha$ is the \redem{linear absorption coefficient} for elastic waves and depends upon the properties of the medium and the wave frequency.
\zhpar{式中 $A_{0}$ 和 $I_{0}$ 分别为 $x = 0$ 处波的振幅和强度，$\alpha$ 为弹性波的\redem{线吸收系数}，它取决于介质的性质和波的频率。}

\subsection{}\label{30.3.8} The \redem{dispersion of waves} is the dependence of the phase velocity of sine waves in a medium on their frequency. A medium in which this phenomenon is observed is said to be a \redem{dispersive medium}. The dispersion of acoustic waves in a boundless medium depends upon the properties of the medium and is always accompanied by the absorption of sound.
\zhpar{\redem{波的色散}是指介质中正弦波的相速度随频率变化的关系。表现出这种现象的介质称为\redem{色散介质}。无界介质中声波的色散取决于介质的性质，并且总是伴随着声音的吸收。}

\section[Superposition of Waves]{Principle of Superposition of Waves. Group Velocity}
\label{sec-30.4} 
\zhtitle{波的叠加原理。群速度}

\subsection{}\label{30.4.1} The \redem{principle of superposition of waves} states that in a linear medium \ssect{30.1.7} waves are propagated independently of one another, so that the resultant disturbance at any point in the medium upon the simultaneous propagation of several waves is equal to the sum of the disturbances corresponding to each of the waves separately.
\zhpar{\redem{波的叠加原理}指出：在线性介质\ssect{30.1.7}中，各列波独立地传播；多列波同时传播时，介质中任一点的合扰动等于各列波单独传播时在该点引起的扰动之和。}

For example, if $n$ different mechanical waves are propagated simultaneously through a linear medium the resultant displacement $\vb{s}$, velocity $\vb{v}$ and acceleration $\vb{a}$ of the particles of the medium at the arbitrary instant $t$ of time are
\zhpar{例如，若 $n$ 列不同的机械波同时在线性介质中传播，则在任一时刻 $t$，介质中质点的合位移 $\vb{s}$、合速度 $\vb{v}$ 和合加速度 $\vb{a}$ 为}
\begin{equation*}%
\vb{s} = \sum_{i}^{n} \vb{s}_{i},\quad \vb{v} = \sum_{i}^{n} \vb{v}_{i},\qand \vb{a} = \sum_{i}^{n} \vb{a}_{i},
\end{equation*}
where $\vb{s}_{i}$, $\vb{v}_{i}$, $\vb{a}_{i}$, and acceleration that the particles being considered would have at the instant of time $t$ if only the $i$-th wave was propagated in the medium.
\zhpar{式中 $\vb{s}_{i}$、$\vb{v}_{i}$、$\vb{a}_{i}$ 分别为：若介质中只传播第 $i$ 列波，则所考虑质点在时刻 $t$ 的位移、速度和加速度。}

\subsection{}\label{30.4.2} On the basis of the principle of wave superposition and a Fourier expansion (\ssect{28.4.6} and \ssect{28.4.7}) any nonsine wave can be replaced by an equivalent system of sine waves, i.e. it can be represented in the form of a \redem{group of waves}, called a \redem{wave train} or packet. The totality of frequency values for these sine waves is called the \redem{frequency spectrum} (or simply \redem{spectrum}) of the nonsine wave. In accordance with the nature of the oscillation excited by the wave, the wave's frequency spectrum can be either discrete \ssect{28.4.6} or continuous \ssect{28.4.7}.
\zhpar{根据波的叠加原理，并利用傅里叶展开\ssect{28.4.6}和\ssect{28.4.7}，任意非正弦波都可以用等效的正弦波系统代替，即表示为一组波，称为\redem{波列}或\redem{波包}。这些正弦波的频率值的全体称为该非正弦波的\redem{频谱}（或简称\redem{谱}）。根据波所激发振动的性质，非正弦波的频谱可以是离散的\ssect{28.4.6}，也可以是连续的\ssect{28.4.7}。}

The law of propagation of an arbitrary disturbance (signal), which is a nonsine wave, in a linear medium is simple only provided that the medium is nondispersive \ssect{30.3.8}. In this case the signal travels in the medium without changing its ``shape'' because all the sine waves making up the wave train have the same phase velocity, which is equal to the velocity of the signal.
\zhpar{任意扰动（即非正弦形式的信号）在线性介质中的传播规律，只有在介质无色散\ssect{30.3.8}的条件下才比较简单。此时，信号在传播过程中不改变其“形状”，因为组成波列的所有正弦波都具有相同的相速度，且等于信号的传播速度。}

\begin{figure}%[-1.5cm]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.8\textwidth]{figs/ch-30/fig-30-02.pdf}
\sidecaption{The ``spread'' of a train wave in a dispersive medium.\zhcap{色散介质中波列的“展宽”。}\label{fig-30-02}}
\end{figure}


In a dispersive medium, the sine components of the wave train, corresponding to the nonsine wave, propagate at different
velocities. Therefore, this wave train ``spreads'' as it propagates, thereby changing the ``shape'' of the signal. If, for example, a signal propagating along the $OX$ axis in a dispersive medium had the ``shape'' shown by dash lines in \figr{fig-30-02} at the instant of time $t_{1}$, then at the instant $t_{2}$ it could have the different ``shape'' shown by a solid line.
\zhpar{在色散介质中，与非正弦波对应的波列的各正弦分量以不同的速度传播。因此，该波列在传播过程中会“展宽”，从而改变信号的“形状”。例如，若沿 $OX$ 轴在色散介质中传播的信号，在时刻 $t_{1}$ 的“形状”如\figr{fig-30-02}中虚线所示，则在时刻 $t_{2}$ 它可能呈现如实线所示的另一种不同的“形状”。}

\subsection{}\label{30.4.3} The simplest wave train is the \redem{plane quasi-sine wave} obtained by the superposition of two plane waves of the same amplitude and with frequencies and wavenumbers close in value propagating along the $OX$ axis. Thus
\zhpar{最简单的波列是由两列振幅相同、频率和波数值相近、沿 $OX$ 轴传播的平面波叠加而成的\redem{平面准正弦波}。即}
\begin{align*}%
s & = A_{0} \sin (\omega t - kx) + A_{0} \sin [(\omega + \dd \omega)\, t - (k + \dd k)\,x] \\
& = 2 A_{0} \cos \left( \frac{t \, \dd \omega - x \, \dd k}{2}\right) \sin (\omega t - kx).
\end{align*}
The dependence of $s$ on $x$ at some fixed instant of time is shown in \figr{fig-30-03}. 
\zhpar{某一固定时刻 $s$ 随 $x$ 的变化关系如\figr{fig-30-03}所示。}

\begin{figure}%[-1.5cm]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.8\textwidth]{figs/ch-30/fig-30-03.pdf}
\sidecaption{The dependence of $s$ on $x$.\zhcap{$s$ 随 $x$ 的变化关系。}\label{fig-30-03}}
\end{figure}

This wave differs from a sine wave in that its amplitude
\zhpar{这种波与正弦波的不同之处在于其振幅}
\begin{equation*}%
A = A_{0} \abs{ \left( \frac{t \, \dd \omega - x \, \dd k}{2}\right)}
\end{equation*}
is a slowly varying function of the coordinate $x$ and time $t$. Taken as the velocity of propagation of this nonsine wave is a velocity $u$ of point $M$ in which the amplitude $A$ has some fixed value (for example, $A = 0$ or $A = 2A_{0}$). Consequently, the law of motion of point $M$ is: $t\, \dd \omega - x\, \dd k = \text{const}.$ From this
\zhpar{是坐标 $x$ 和时间 $t$ 的缓变函数。把振幅取某一固定值（例如 $A = 0$ 或 $A = 2A_{0}$）的点 $M$ 的速度 $u$ 作为该非正弦波的传播速度。因此，点 $M$ 的运动规律为 $t\, \dd \omega - x\, \dd k = \text{const}.$ 由此得}
\begin{equation*}%
u = \dv{x}{t} = \dv{\omega}{k}.
\end{equation*}
Velocity $u$ is called the \redem{group velocity}. It is equal to the velocity of energy transfer by a quasi-sine wave. The group velocity $u = \dv*{\omega}{k}$ is suitable for describing energy transfer (signal transmission) by nonsine waves having a different frequency spectrum provided that the spectrum is not very wide and that the dispersion of waves in the medium at these frequencies is not too great.
\zhpar{速度 $u$ 称为\redem{群速度}，它等于准正弦波传递能量的速度。群速度 $u = \dv*{\omega}{k}$ 适用于描述具有不同频谱的非正弦波的能量传递（信号传输），前提是频谱不太宽，并且介质在这些频率下的色散不太强。}

The relation between the group velocity $(u = \dv*{\omega}{k})$ and the phase velocity $(v = \omega/k)$ of waves is of the form:
\zhpar{群速度 $(u = \dv*{\omega}{k})$ 与波的相速度 $(v = \omega/k)$ 之间的关系为：}
\begin{equation*}%
u = v - \lambda \dv{v}{\lambda},
\end{equation*}
where $\lambda$ is the wavelength \ssect{30.2.6}. In a nondispersive medium $\dv*{v}{\lambda} = 0$ and the group and phase velocities coincide.
\zhpar{式中 $\lambda$ 为波长\ssect{30.2.6}。在无色散介质中 $\dv*{v}{\lambda} = 0$，群速度与相速度相等。}


\section[Interference of Waves]{Interference of Waves. Standing Waves}
\label{sec-30.5} 
\zhtitle{波的干涉。驻波}


\begin{marginfigure}%[-1.5cm]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{figs/ch-30/fig-30-04.pdf}
\sidecaption{Superposing of two incoherent sine waves.\zhcap{两个非相干简谐波的叠加。}\label{fig-30-04}}
\end{marginfigure}

\subsection{}\label{30.5.1} Two waves are said to be \redem{coherent} if their phase difference is independent of time. Coherent waves correspond to coherent oscillations (or vibrations) \ssect{28.4.3}. The sources of co­ herent waves are called \redem{coherent sources}. Sine waves of equal frequency are always coherent. Waves of different frequencies are coherent only for the length of time that the oscillations (or vibra­ tions) they excite are co­ herent \ssect{28.4.4}. 
\zhpar{若两列波的相位差不随时间变化，则称这两列波为\redem{相干波}。相干波对应于相干振动\ssect{28.4.3}。产生相干波的波源称为\redem{相干波源}。同频率的简谐波总是相干的。不同频率的波只有在它们所激发的振动保持相干的时间内才是相干的\ssect{28.4.4}。}

In superposing incoherent sine waves, generated by point sources $S_{1}$ and $S_{2}$ (\figr{fig-30-04}), the square of the amplitude $A$ of the resultant nonharmonic vibration at arbitrary point $M$ varies periodically with time $t$ according to the relation (see \ssect{28.4.2} and \ssect{30.2.8}):
\zhpar{由点波源 $S_{1}$ 和 $S_{2}$ 产生的非相干简谐波（见\figr{fig-30-04}）叠加时，介质中任一点 $M$ 处合成的非简谐振动振幅 $A$ 的平方随时间 $t$ 周期性地变化，其关系为（参见\ssect{28.4.2}和\ssect{30.2.8}）：}
\begin{align*}%
A^{2} & = A_{1}^{2} + A_{2}^{2} \\
& + 2A_{1}A_{2} \cos [(\omega_{2} -  \omega_{1}t - (k_{2}r_{2} - k_{1}r_{1}) + (\alpha_{2} - \alpha_{1})].
\end{align*}
Here $A_{1}$ and $A_{2}$, and $k_{1}$, and $k_{2}$, and $\alpha_{1}$ and $\alpha_{2}$ are the amplitudes at point $M$, angular velocities, wavenumbers and the initial phases of the two spherical waves being superposed. 
\zhpar{式中 $A_{1}$、$A_{2}$、$k_{1}$、$k_{2}$、$\alpha_{1}$ 和 $\alpha_{2}$ 分别为被叠加的两个球面波在 $M$ 点处的振幅、角频率、波数和初相位。}

The period of variation of $A_{2}$ is equal to $T= 2\pi/\abs{\omega_{2} - \omega_{1}}$. The average value of the square of the amplitude during the period is
\zhpar{振幅的变化周期为 $T= 2\pi/\abs{\omega_{2} - \omega_{1}}$。振幅平方在一个周期内的平均值为}
\begin{equation*}%
\expval{A^{2}} = A_{1}^{2} + A_{2}^{2}.
\end{equation*}
In the superposition of incoherent waves, the squares of their amplitudes are added together.
\zhpar{在非相干波的叠加中，各列波的振幅平方相加。}

\subsection{}\label{30.5.2} The \redem{interference of waves} is the phenomenon, in super­ posing waves, of their mutual strengthening, stable with time, at certain points in space and their weakening at others, depend­ ing upon the relations between the phases of these waves.
\zhpar{\redem{波的干涉}是指波叠加时，由于各波之间的相位关系，空间某些点上的振动相互加强、另一些点上的振动相互减弱，并且这种加强与减弱在时间上保持稳定的现象。}

Only coherent waves display interference, provided that they correspond to vibrations in the same or almost the same direction.
\zhpar{只有相干波、且对应的振动方向相同或近似相同时，才会出现干涉现象。}

In superposing coherent spherical waves, generated by the point sources $S_{1}$ and $S_{2}$ (\figr{fig-30-04}),
\zhpar{由点波源 $S_{1}$ 和 $S_{2}$ 产生的相干球面波（见\figr{fig-30-04}）叠加时，}
\begin{align*}%
s_{1} & = A_{1} \sin(\omega t  - kr_{1}+ \alpha_{1})= A_{1}\sin
\Phi_{1} \qand\\
s_{2} & = A_{2} \sin(\omega t  - kr_{2}+ \alpha_{2})= A_{2}\sin
\Phi_{2};
\end{align*}
amplitude $A$ and phase $\Phi$ of the resultant harmonic vibrations at point $M$ (where $s = s_{1}+ s_{2} = A \sin \Phi$) are determined from the relations \ssect{28.4.3}:
\zhpar{介质中点 $M$ 处合成简谐振动的振幅 $A$ 和相位 $\Phi$（其中 $s = s_{1}+ s_{2} = A \sin \Phi$）由下列关系确定（参见\ssect{28.4.3}）：}
\begin{align*}%
A^{2} & = A_{1}^{2} + A_{2}^{2} + 2 A_{1}A_{2} \cos [k(r_{2} - r_{1}) - (\alpha_{2} - \alpha_{1})] \qand \\
\tan \Phi & = \frac{A_{1}\sin \Phi_{1} + A_{2}\sin \Phi_{2}}{A_{1}\cos \Phi_{1} + A_{2}\sin \Phi_{2}}. 
\end{align*}
Since the difference of the initial phases $\alpha_{2} \alpha_{1}= \text{const.}$ for the vibration of the coherent sources $S_{2}$ and $S_{1}$ the resulting interference of the two waves at various points $M$ depends on the quantity $\Delta = r_{2} - r_{1}$, called the \redem{path difference of the waves}. At interference maxima, the amplitude of the resultant vibration is $A = A_{1}+ A_{2}$, whereas at interference minima $A = \abs{A_{1}- A_{2}}$. Maxima are observed at the points $M$ that
comply with the condition 
\zhpar{由于两相干波源 $S_{2}$ 与 $S_{1}$ 的振动初相位之差 $\alpha_{2} \alpha_{1}= \text{const.}$，两列波在各点 $M$ 处的干涉结果取决于波程差 $\Delta = r_{2} - r_{1}$（称为\redem{波程差}）。干涉极大处合成振动的振幅为 $A = A_{1}+ A_{2}$，干涉极小处为 $A = \abs{A_{1}- A_{2}}$。在满足下列条件的点 $M$ 处出现干涉极大：}
\begin{equation*}%
k \Delta - (\alpha_{2} - \alpha_{1}) = \pm 2m \pi,
\end{equation*}
where $m = 0, \,1,\, 2,\, \ldots{\,}$ is the \redem{order of the interference maximum}. 
\zhpar{式中 $m = 0, \,1,\, 2,\, \ldots{\,}$ 为\redem{干涉极大的级次}。}

The condition for interference minima is of the form
\zhpar{干涉极小的条件为如下形式：}
\begin{equation*}%
k\Delta - (\alpha_{2} - \alpha_{1}) = \pm (2m -1) \pi,
\end{equation*}
where $m = 0, \,1,\, 2,\, \ldots{\,}$ is the \redem{order of the interference minimum}. Since the wavenumber $k = 2\pi/\lambda$, where $\lambda$ is the wavelength in the given medium, the conditions for interference maxima and minima can be presented in the form:
\zhpar{式中 $m = 0, \,1,\, 2,\, \ldots{\,}$ 为\redem{干涉极小的级次}。由于波数 $k = 2\pi/\lambda$，其中 $\lambda$ 为给定介质中的波长，干涉极大和极小的条件可以写成如下形式：}
\begin{align*}%
\Delta & = \pm m \lambda + \frac{\alpha_{2} - \alpha_{1}}{2 \pi} \lambda \,\, \text{(maxima)}, \\
\Delta & = \pm (2m -1) \frac{\lambda}{2} + \frac{\alpha_{2} - \alpha_{1}}{2 \pi} \lambda \,\, \text{(minima)}.
\end{align*}
Finally, when $\alpha_{2} = \alpha_{1}$ the conditions take the form:
\zhpar{最后，当 $\alpha_{2} = \alpha_{1}$ 时，上述条件变为：}
\begin{align*}%
\Delta & = \pm m \lambda \,\, \text{(maxima)}, \\
\Delta & = \pm (2m -1)  \,\, \text{(minima)}.
\end{align*}
On the straight line $ab$, parallel to and at the distance $L$ from the line passing through the sources $S_{1}$ and $S_{2}$, (\figr{fig-30-04}), the central maximum of zero order is at point $O$, equidistant from sources $S_{1}$ and $S_{2}$. If the distance between the sourcea $l \ll L$ then for point $M$ on line $ab$, at the distance $z \ll L$ from point $O$, the path difference of the waves is
\zhpar{在与穿过波源 $S_{1}$ 和 $S_{2}$ 的直线平行、且距其为 $L$ 的直线 $ab$ 上（见\figr{fig-30-04}），零级中央极大位于与 $S_{1}$、$S_{2}$ 等距的 $O$ 点。若两波源之间的距离 $l \ll L$，则对于直线 $ab$ 上距 $O$ 点为 $z \ll L$ 的点 $M$，波程差为}
\begin{equation*}%
\Delta = \frac{lz}{L}.
\end{equation*}
Maxima of the $m$th and $(m + 1)$th orders correspond to the values
\zhpar{第 $m$ 级和第 $(m + 1)$ 级极大对应的位置分别为}
\begin{align*}%
z_{m} & = \frac{m \lambda L}{l} \qand \\
z_{m + 1} & = \frac{(m + 1) \lambda L}{l},
\end{align*}
so that the difference between adjacent maxima equals $\lambda L/l$. 
\zhpar{因此，相邻两极大之间的距离等于 $\lambda L/l$。}

\subsection{}\label{30.5.3} When waves interfere, their energies cannot be simply added together. At interference maxima, the intensity of the resultant wave is greater than the sum of the intensities of the superposed waves, and at minima, it is less than their sum. Wave interference leads to a redistribution of vibrational energy between neighbouring regions of the medium. But on an average, for a large region of space, the energy of the resultant wave is equal to the sum of the energies of the interfering waves. This follows from the laws of conservation and conversion of energy. 
\zhpar{波的干涉过程中，能量不能简单相加。在干涉极大处，合成波的强度大于各叠加波强度之和；在干涉极小处，则小于它们的和。波的干涉会导致相邻区域之间振动能量的重新分配。但就较大的空间区域而言，平均来说，合成波的能量等于各干涉波的能量之和。这一结论来自能量守恒与转化定律。}

\subsection{}\label{30.5.4} A special case of wave interference is standing waves. A \redem{standing wave} is one that is formed by the superposition of two travelling sine waves that are propagating toward each other and have the same frequency and amplitude. In the case of transverse waves, they also have the same polarization \ssect{30.2.2}. A standing wave is formed, for instance, in a stretched flexible string when one end is fixed and vibratory motion is imparted to the other end.
\zhpar{\redem{驻波}是波干涉的一种特殊情形。驻波由两列频率和振幅相同、沿相反方向相向传播的行波叠加而成。对于横波，两列波的偏振也相同\ssect{30.2.2}。例如，当张紧的柔软弦一端固定、另一端做振动时，弦中就会形成驻波。}

In the superposition of two coherent travelling plane waves of the form
\zhpar{两列相干平面行波的叠加形式为}
\begin{align*}%
s_{1} & = A \sin (\omega t - kx) \qand \\
s_{2} & = A \sin (\omega t + kx + \alpha),
\end{align*}
where $\alpha$ is the phase difference at points $x = 0$, a \redem{plane standing wave} is formed. It is described by the equation
\zhpar{式中 $\alpha$ 为 $x = 0$ 处两波的相位差，叠加后形成\redem{平面驻波}，其方程为：}
\begin{equation*}%
s = s_{1} + s_{2} = 2A\cos \left(kx + \frac{\alpha}{2} \right) \, \sin \left( \omega t + \frac{\alpha}{2} \right).
\end{equation*}
In contrast to the amplitude $A$ of travelling waves, the \redem{amplitude $A_{st}$ of a standing wave} is a periodic function of the coordinate x:
\zhpar{与行波的振幅 $A$ 不同，\redem{驻波的振幅 $A_{st}$} 是坐标 $x$ 的周期函数：}
\begin{equation*}%
A_{st} = 2A \abs{\cos \left(kx + \frac{\alpha}{2} \right)}.
\end{equation*}

\subsection{}\label{30.5.5} Points of a standing wave at which $A_{st} = 0$ are called its \redem{nodes}, points at which the amplitude $A_{st}$ reaches its maximum value ($A_{st} = 2A$) are called the \redem{antinodes}. The positions of the nodes and antinodes are determined from the conditions:
\zhpar{驻波中振幅 $A_{st} = 0$ 的点称为其\redem{波节}，振幅 $A_{st}$ 达到最大值（$A_{st} = 2A$）的点称为\redem{波腹}。波节和波腹的位置由下列条件确定：}
\begin{align*}%
kx + \frac{\alpha}{2} = (2m + 1) \frac{\pi}{2} \,\, \text{nodes,}\\
kx + \frac{\alpha}{2} = m \pi \frac{\pi}{2} \,\, \text{antinodes,}
\end{align*}
where $m = 0,\,1,\,2,\ldots{\,}$.
\zhpar{式中 $m = 0,\,1,\,2,\ldots{\,}$。}

The distances between two adjacent nodes and between two adjacent antinodes are the same and equal to half the wavelength  $\lambda$ of the travelling waves. This distance is called the \redem{length of the standing wave}: $\lambda_{st} = \lambda/2$. The distance between adjacent nodes and antinodes of a standing wave is equal to $\lambda_{st}/2$.
\zhpar{相邻两波节之间的距离与相邻两波腹之间的距离相等，且都等于行波波长 $\lambda$ 的一半。此距离称为驻波的长度：$\lambda_{st} = \lambda/2$。驻波中相邻波节与波腹之间的距离等于 $\lambda_{st}/2$。}

\subsection{}\label{30.5.6} In a travelling wave the phase of the vibration depends upon the coordinate $x$ of the point being considered. In a standing wave all the points between two nodes vibrate with differ­ ent amplitudes, but in the \redem{same} phase (cophased) because the argument of the sine in the equation of a standing wave \ssect{30.5.4} is independent of the coordinate $x$. In passing through a node, the phase of the vibration changes abruptly by $\pi$, so that the sign of $\cos (kx + \alpha/2)$ is reversed.
\zhpar{在行波中，振动的相位取决于所考虑点的坐标 $x$。而在驻波中，两波节之间的所有点振幅各不相同，但\redem{相位相同}（同相），因为驻波方程中正弦函数的自变量与坐标 $x$ 无关。通过波节时，振动相位突变 $\pi$，因此 $\cos (kx + \alpha/2)$ 的符号发生反转。}

Illustrated in \figr{fig-30-05} is the nature of the motion of various points on a stretched flexible string of length $l$ when a transverse standing wave is established in it. The left end $O$ of the string is set into harmonic motion and the right end $N$ is fixed.
\zhpar{\figr{fig-30-05}展示了长度为 $l$ 的张紧柔软弦中建立横向驻波时，弦上各点的运动情况。弦的左端 $O$ 做简谐振动，右端 $N$ 固定。}

\begin{figure}%[-1.5cm]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.8\textwidth]{figs/ch-30/fig-30-05.pdf}
\sidecaption{The nature of motion of various points on a stretched flexible string when a transverse wave is established in it.\zhcap{张紧柔软弦中建立横波时各点的运动情况。}\label{fig-30-05}}
\end{figure}


In this case, as the wave is reflected from the place where the string is fixed, its phase is changed by $\pi$, so that a node of the standing wave is formed at this point. The phase difference of the reflected and incident waves at point $O$ (where $x = 0$) is $\alpha = - (2kl + \pi)$. The nodes of the standing wave are designated by small circles in \figr{fig-30-05}, and the instant of time $t_{0}$ is selected so that $\sin (\omega t_{0} + \alpha/2) = 0$.
\zhpar{在这种情形下，波在弦的固定端反射时相位改变 $\pi$，因而该点形成驻波的波节。反射波与入射波在 $O$ 点（$x = 0$）的相位差为 $\alpha = - (2kl + \pi)$。\figr{fig-30-05}中用小圆圈标出驻波的波节，并选取时刻 $t_{0}$ 使 $\sin (\omega t_{0} + \alpha/2) = 0$。}

\subsection{}\label{30.5.7} In a standing wave \ssect{30.5.4}, the velocity of vibratory motion of the particles of the medium is
\zhpar{在驻波\ssect{30.5.4}中，介质质点的振动速度为}
\begin{equation*}%
v_{1} = \pdv{s}{t} = 2A \omega \cos \left(kx + \frac{\alpha}{2} \right) \cos \left( \omega t + \frac{\alpha}{2} \right),
\end{equation*}
and the relative deformation of the medium is 
\zhpar{而介质的相对形变为}
\begin{align*}%
\varepsilon  & = \pdv{s}{k} =  -2Ak\sin \left( kx + \frac{\alpha}{2} \right) \sin \left( \omega t + \frac{\alpha}{2} \right)\\
& = 2 Ak \sin \left( kx + \frac{\alpha}{2} \right) \cos \left( \omega t + \frac{\alpha}{2} + \frac{\pi}{2} \right).
\end{align*}
Thus, in contrast to a travelling wave \ssect{30.3.4}, in a standing wave $\varepsilon$ has a phase lead of $\pi/2$ over $v_{1}$. Consequently, at the instants of time when reaches its amplitude value, $\varepsilon$ becomes zero and vice versa. Moreover, the amplitudes of $v_{1}$ and $\varepsilon$ depend upon the coordinate $x$, but in different ways. Thus, the anti­ nodes of the particle velocity and nodes of deformation of the medium are at the antinodes of the standing wave \ssect{30.5.5}, whereas the antinodes of the deformation and the nodes of the velocity are at the nodes of the standing wave.
\zhpar{因此，与行波\ssect{30.3.4}不同，驻波中 $\varepsilon$ 比 $v_{1}$ 超前 $\pi/2$ 的相位。于是，当 $v_{1}$ 达到其振幅值时，$\varepsilon$ 为零，反之亦然。此外，$v_{1}$ 和 $\varepsilon$ 的振幅随坐标 $x$ 的变化方式也不同：质点速度的波腹和介质形变的波节位于驻波的波腹处，而形变的波腹和速度的波节位于驻波的波节处。}

In an elastic standing wave, the energy is periodically converted from potential energy, localized mainly near the deformation antinodes, into kinetic energy, localized mainly near the velocity antinodes, and back again. Therefore, energy periodically migrates from the nodes of a standing wave to its antinodes and back again. But in the nodes and antinodes themselves the energy flux density is identically equal to zero. The average value of the energy flux density over one period is equal to zero at any point on a standing wave, because the two travelling waves that form the standing wave transmit equal amounts of energy over a period in opposite directions. This is why they have been called standing waves.
\zhpar{在弹性驻波中，能量周期性地在势能（主要集中在形变波腹附近）与动能（主要集中在速度波腹附近）之间相互转换，因此能量也周期性地在驻波的波节与波腹之间往复迁移。但在波节和波腹处，能流密度恒等于零。驻波上任一点的能流密度在一个周期内的平均值都等于零，因为构成驻波的两列行波在一个周期内沿相反方向输送的能量相等。这正是它们被称为驻波的原因。}

\subsection{}\label{30.5.8} In the free vibrations of strings, rods and columns of gas, standing waves are set up in them. The frequencies of these waves comply with definite conditions, i.e. they can have only certain discrete values, which are called the \redem{natural frequencies of vibration} of the corresponding vibratory system. Displacement nodes (deformation antinodes) are located at the rigidly fixed ends of strings or rods, and displacement antinodes (defor­ mation nodes) are located at the free ends of the rods. In the vibrations of a cylindrical column of gas in a pipe, a pressure antinode is located at the closed end of the pipe and a pressure node at the open end.
\zhpar{在弦、杆和气柱的自由振动中，其内部会形成驻波。这些波的频率须满足一定条件，只能取某些离散值，称为相应振动系统的\redem{固有振动频率}。位移波节（形变波腹）位于弦或杆的刚性固定端，位移波腹（形变波节）位于杆的自由端。在管中圆柱形气柱的振动中，压强波腹位于管的封闭端，压强波节位于管的开口端。}

If $l$ is the length of the string, rod or gas column, $v$ is the phase velocity of the wave and $\lambda$ is the wavelength, then for strings and rods fixed at both ends and for gas columns in pipes that are open or closed at both ends, a whole number of lengths $\lambda_{st} = \lambda/2$ of the standing wave fits into the length $l$, i.e.
\zhpar{若 $l$ 为弦、杆或气柱的长度，$v$ 为波的相速度，$\lambda$ 为波长，则对于两端固定的弦和杆，以及两端开口或两端封闭的气柱，驻波长度 $\lambda_{st} = \lambda/2$ 的整数倍恰好与长度 $l$ 相吻合，即}
\begin{equation*}%
l = m \lambda_{st} = \frac{m \lambda}{2}, 
\end{equation*}
where $m = 1,\,2,\,3,\ldots{\,}$.
\zhpar{式中 $m = 1,\,2,\,3,\ldots{\,}$。}

The natural frequency of vibrations of such systems is
\zhpar{这类系统的固有振动频率为}
\begin{equation*}%
\nu = \frac{mv}{2l}.
\end{equation*}
For rods fixed at one end and free at the other, or for pipes closed at one end and open at the other,
\zhpar{对于一端固定、另一端自由的杆，或一端封闭、另一端开口的管，}
\begin{equation*}%
l = (2m - 1) \frac{\lambda_{st}}{2} = (2m - 1) \frac{\lambda}{4},
\end{equation*}
and the natural frequency of vibration is
\zhpar{固有振动频率为}
\begin{equation*}%
\nu = \frac{(2m -1)v}{4l}.
\end{equation*}

\section{Doppler Effect in Acoustics}
\label{sec-30.6} 
\zhtitle{声学中的多普勒效应}
\begin{marginfigure}[-2.5cm]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{figs/ch-30/fig-30-06.pdf}
\sidecaption{Analysis of the Doppler effect due to motion of the source and the detector.\zhcap{由波源与探测器运动引起的多普勒效应分析。}\label{fig-30-06}}
\end{marginfigure}


\subsection{}\label{30.6.1} The \redem{Doppler effect} refers to the change in the frequency of waves, observed or recorded by a receiver, or detector, which is due to motion of the source of the waves and the detector (or observer). For example, as a rapid train approaches a stationary ob­ server, the pitch of the loco­ motive whistle is higher, and as the train passes the observer and travels away it is lower, than the pitch of the same whistle when the train is standing still. 
\zhpar{\redem{多普勒效应}是指接收器（或探测器）所观测或记录到的波的频率，因波源与探测器的运动而发生变化的现象。例如，一列快速行驶的火车驶向静止的观察者时，机车汽笛的音调比火车静止时高；火车驶过观察者并远离时，音调则比静止时低。}



Assume that the detector $D$ of acoustic waves in a gas­ eous (or liquid) medium is fixed with respect to the medium, and that the source S is moving away at the velocity along a straight line joining them (\figr{fig-30-06}~\ired{(a)}). During the period $T_{0}$ of its vibration, the source travels in the medium a
distance of $v_{1}T_{0} = v_{1}/\nu_{0}$, where $\nu_{0}$ is the source's fre­ quency of vibration. Therefore, upon motion of the source the wavelength $\lambda$ in the medium differs from its value $\lambda_{0}$ when the source is stationary:
\zhpar{设气体（或液体）中声波的探测器 $D$ 相对于介质静止，波源 $S$ 沿两者的连线以速度 $v_{1}$ 远离探测器运动（见\figr{fig-30-06}~\ired{(a)}）。在波源振动的一个周期 $T_{0}$ 内，波源在介质中移动的距离为 $v_{1}T_{0} = v_{1}/\nu_{0}$，其中 $\nu_{0}$ 为波源的振动频率。因此，波源运动时介质中的波长 $\lambda$ 与波源静止时的波长 $\lambda_{0}$ 不同：}
\begin{equation*}%
\lambda = \lambda_{0} + v_{1} T_{0} = ( v + v_{1} ) T_{0} =\frac{( v + v_{1} )}{\nu_{0}},
\end{equation*} 
where $v$ is the phase velocity of the wave in the medium. The frequency of the wave recorded by the detector is
\zhpar{式中 $v$ 为介质中波的相速度。探测器记录到的波频率为}
\begin{equation*}%
\nu = \frac{v}{\lambda}  = \frac{\nu_{0}}{1 + v_{1}/v}.
\end{equation*}
If the velocity vector of the source makes the arbitrary angle 
$\theta_{1}$ with the radius vector $\vb{R}$, connecting the stationary detector to the source (\figr{fig-30-06}~\ired{(b)}), then
\zhpar{若波源的速度矢量与连接静止探测器和波源的矢径 $\vb{R}$ 成任意夹角 $\theta_{1}$（见\figr{fig-30-06}~\ired{(b)}），则}
\begin{equation*}%
\nu = \frac{\nu_{0}}{1 + v_{1} \cos \theta_{1}/v}.
\end{equation*}

\subsection{}\label{30.6.2} When the source is stationary and the detector approaches it at the velocity $\vb{v}_{2}$ along a straight line joining them (\figr{fig-30-06}~\ired{(c)}), the wavelength in the medium is $\lambda = \lambda_{0} = v/\nu_{0}$. But the velocity of propagation of the wave with respect to the detector is $v + v_{2}$. Hence, the wave frequency recorded by the detector is
\zhpar{当波源静止、探测器以速度 $\vb{v}_{2}$ 沿两者的连线接近波源时（见\figr{fig-30-06}~\ired{(c)}），介质中的波长为 $\lambda = \lambda_{0} = v/\nu_{0}$。但波相对于探测器的传播速度为 $v + v_{2}$。因此，探测器记录到的波频率为}
\begin{equation*}%
\nu = \frac{v + v_{2}} {\lambda_{0}} = \nu_{0} \left( 1 + \frac{v_{2}}{v}\right).
\end{equation*}
In the case when the velocity vector $\vb{v}_{2}$ makes the arbitrary angle $\theta_{2}$ with radius vector $\vb{R}$, connecting the travelling detector to the stationary source (\figr{fig-30-06}~\ired{(d)}),
\zhpar{若速度矢量 $\vb{v}_{2}$ 与连接运动探测器和静止波源的矢径 $\vb{R}$ 成任意夹角 $\theta_{2}$（见\figr{fig-30-06}~\ired{(d)}），则}
\begin{equation*}%
\nu = \nu_{0} \left( 1 + \frac{v_{2}}{v} \cos \theta_{2} \right).
\end{equation*}

\subsection{}\label{30.6.3} In the most general case, when both the detector and source of acoustic waves are travelling with respect to the medium at arbitrary velocities (\figr{fig-30-06}~\ired{(e)}),
\zhpar{在最一般的情形下，即声波的探测器和波源相对于介质均以任意速度运动（见\figr{fig-30-06}~\ired{(e)}）时，}
\begin{equation*}%
\nu = \nu_{0} \frac{1 + \dfrac{v_{2}}{v} \cos \theta_{2}}{1 + \dfrac{v_{1}}{v} \cos \theta_{1}}.
\end{equation*}
This formula can also be presented in the form
\zhpar{该式也可以写成如下形式：}
\begin{equation*}
\nu = \nu_{0} \left( 1 - \frac{V \cos \theta}{v} \left[ 1 - \frac{v_{1}}{v} \cos \theta_{1} + \left( \frac{v_{1}}{v} \cos \theta_{1} \right)^{2} - \ldots \right] \right),
\end{equation*}
where $\vb{V} = \vb{v}_{1} - \vb{v}_{2}$ is the velocity of the wave source with respect to the detector, and $\theta$ is the angle between vectors $\vb{V}$ and $\vb{R}$. The quantity $V \cos \theta$, equal to the projection of $\vb{V}$ on the direction of $\vb{R}$, is called the \redem{ray velocity of the source}.
\zhpar{式中 $\vb{V} = \vb{v}_{1} - \vb{v}_{2}$ 为波源相对于探测器的速度，$\theta$ 为矢量 $\vb{V}$ 与 $\vb{R}$ 之间的夹角。量 $V \cos \theta$ 等于 $\vb{V}$ 在 $\vb{R}$ 方向上的投影，称为\redem{波源的射线速度}。}

When $v_{1} \ll v$,
\zhpar{当 $v_{1} \ll v$ 时，}
\begin{equation*}%
\nu \approx \nu_{0} \left(1 - \frac{V \cos \theta}{v} \right).
\end{equation*}
