\section{Huygens-Fresnel Principle}
\label{sec-33.1} 
\zhtitle{惠更斯-菲涅耳原理}

\subsection{}\label{33.1.1} If the position of the wavefront \ssect{30.2.3} is known at a certain instant $t$ of time, as well as the velocity $v$ of the wave, the position of the wavefront at the subsequent instant $t + \Delta t$ can
he determined on the basis of the \redem{Huygens principle}. According to this principle, each point on the surface $S (t)$, through which the wavefront passes at the instant $t$ can be regarded as a source of secondary waves, and the sought-for position $S (t + \Delta t)$ of I he wavefront at the instant of time $t + \Delta t$ coincides with the envelope of all the secondary waves at this later time. It is assumed here that in a homogeneous medium the secondary waves radiate only forward, i.e. in directions making acute angles with the outward normals to the wavefront. In a homogeneous isotropic medium, the secondary waves are spherical (\figr{fig-33-01}).
\zhpar{若已知某一时刻 $t$ 的波前位置（\ssect{30.2.3}）以及波速 $v$，则可以根据\redem{惠更斯原理}确定随后时刻 $t + \Delta t$ 的波前位置。依据该原理，在时刻 $t$ 波前所经过的曲面 $S (t)$ 上的每一点都可视为一个次波源，而时刻 $t + \Delta t$ 的波前位置 $S (t + \Delta t)$ 与这些次波在该时刻的包络面相重合。这里假定，在均匀介质中次波只向前辐射，即只沿与波前外法线成锐角的方向传播。在均匀各向同性介质中，次波为球面波（见\figr{fig-33-01}）。}


\subsection{}\label{33.1.2} The Huygens principle can be used to derive laws for the reflection and refraction of light at the interface between two media. The line $MN$ in \figr{fig-33-02} represents the plane interface between two media in which the velocities of light equal $v_{1}$ and $v_{2}$. Incident on this interface at the angle $i$ is a plane wave (rays $\mathit{1}$ and $\mathit{2}$). At the instant $t$ of time the wavefront (plane $AB$) reaches the interface at point $A$. Hence point $A$ begins to radiate secondary waves, propagating both in the first medium
(reflected wave) and in the second medium (transmitted wave).
\zhpar{惠更斯原理可用于推导光在两种介质分界面上反射和折射的定律。在\figr{fig-33-02}中，直线 $MN$ 表示两种介质的平面界面，两种介质中的光速分别为 $v_{1}$ 和 $v_{2}$。以角度 $i$ 入射到该界面上的是平面波（光线 $\mathit{1}$ 和 $\mathit{2}$）。在时刻 $t$，波前（平面 $AB$）在点 $A$ 处到达界面。因此，点 $A$ 开始辐射次波，次波既在第一种介质中传播（反射波），也在第二种介质中传播（透射波）。}

\begin{figure}%[-3cm]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.8\textwidth]{figs/ch-33/fig-33-02.pdf}
\sidecaption{Analysing reflection and refraction using Huygen's principle.\zhcap{利用惠更斯原理分析反射与折射。}\label{fig-33-02}}
\end{figure}

During the time $\Delta t$ required for the incident wave to travel the distance $\overline{BC}$ (where $\Delta t = \overline{BC}/v_{1}$), the front of the secondary wave, radiated from point $A$, reaches points of a semisphere in
the first medium having the radius $R_{1} = v_{1} \Delta t = \overline{BC}$, and points of a semisphere in the second medium having the radius $R_{2} = v_{2} \Delta t = v_{2} \overline{BC}/v_{1}$. The front ol the reflected wave (rays $\mathit{1}'$ and $\mathit{2}'$, propagating at the angle $i'$ of reflection is the plane $DC$, tangent to a sphere of radius $R_{1}$ with its centre at point $A$.
\zhpar{在时间 $\Delta t$ 内（$\Delta t = \overline{BC}/v_{1}$），入射波传播了距离 $\overline{BC}$。从点 $A$ 辐射的次波的波前，到达第一种介质中半径为 $R_{1} = v_{1} \Delta t = \overline{BC}$ 的半球面上的各点，以及第二种介质中半径为 $R_{2} = v_{2} \Delta t = v_{2} \overline{BC}/v_{1}$ 的半球面上的各点。以反射角 $i'$ 传播的反射波波前（光线 $\mathit{1}'$ 和 $\mathit{2}'$）是平面 $DC$，它与以点 $A$ 为中心、半径为 $R_{1}$ 的球面相切。}

Correspondingly, the front of the transmitted (refracted) wave
(rays $\mathit{1}''$ and $\mathit{2}''$), propagating at the refraction angle $r$, is the plane $CE$, tangent to a semisphere of radius $R_{2}$ with its centre at point $A$. The \redem{law of light reflection}: 
\zhpar{以折射角 $r$ 传播的透射波（光线 $\mathit{1}''$ 和 $\mathit{2}''$）的波前是平面 $CE$，它与以点 $A$ 为中心、半径为 $R_{2}$ 的半球面相切。\redem{光的反射定律}为：}
\begin{equation*}%
i = i', 
\end{equation*}
follows from the equality of triangles $ACD$ and $ACB$. The law of light refraction follows from the rectangular triangles $ACB$ and $ACE$, which have a common hypotenuse. Thus
\zhpar{上式可由三角形 $ACD$ 与 $ACB$ 相等得出。光的折射定律则由具有公共斜边的直角三角形 $ACB$ 与 $ACE$ 得出。于是}
\begin{equation*}%
\frac{\sin i}{\sin r} = \frac{\overline{BC}}{\overline{AE}} = \frac{v_{1}}{v_{2}} = n_{21}, 
\end{equation*}
where $n_{21}$ is the relative refractive index of the second and first media \ssect{31.5.1}.
\zhpar{式中 $n_{21}$ 为第二种介质相对于第一种介质的相对折射率（\ssect{31.5.1}）。}


\subsection{}\label{33.1.3} The Huygens principle is a purely geometrical one. It indicates no method of calculating the amplitude of the wave that is an envelope of the secondary waves. Therefore, the Huygens principle is insufficient for deriving the laws of propaga-tion of light waves. An approximate method of solving this problem, which is a development of the Huygens principle on the basis of the idea proposed by Fresnel on the coherence of secondary waves and their interference upon superposition is called the \redem{Huygens-Fresnel principle}. This principle can be
expressed by the following series of propositions:
\zhpar{惠更斯原理纯属几何方法，它没有给出计算次波包络面上振幅的方法。因此，仅凭惠更斯原理不足以推导光波的传播规律。在惠更斯原理的基础上，结合菲涅耳提出的次波相干性及其叠加时相互干涉的思想，得到一种求解这一问题的近似方法，称为\redem{惠更斯-菲涅耳原理}。该原理可以概括为以下几条命题：}
\begin{enumerate}[label = (\alph*)]
\item In calculating the amplitude of light vibration, set up by source $S_{0}$, at arbitrary point $M$, the source can be replaced by a system of secondary sources. These sources are small portions $\dd s$ of any closed auxiliary surface $S$ arranged so that it encloses the source $S_{0}$, but does not enclose point $M$ being considered.
\zhpar{计算光源 $S_{0}$ 在任意点 $M$ 处所产生的光振动振幅时，可以用一组次波源代替该光源。这些次波源是任意闭合辅助曲面 $S$ 上的小面元 $\dd s$，该曲面包围光源 $S_{0}$，但不包围所考虑的点 $M$。}
\item The secondary sources are coherent with $S_{0}$ and with one another. Hence, the secondary waves they set up interfere upon superposition. Interference calculations are simplest when $S$ is the wavefront for the light from source $S_{0}$ because, in this case, the phases of vibration of all secondary sources are the same.
\zhpar{次波源与 $S_{0}$ 以及彼此之间都是相干的，因此它们所产生的次波在叠加时相互干涉。当 $S$ 为光源 $S_{0}$ 发出的光的波前时，干涉计算最为简单，因为此时所有次波源的振动相位都相同。}
\item The amplitude $\dd A$ of the vibration set up at point $M$ by a secondary source is proportional to the ratio of the area $\dd s$ of the corresponding portion of wavefront $S$ to the distance $r$ from this portion to point $M$ and depends upon the angle $\alpha$ between the outward normal to the wavefront and the direction of a line from the element $\dd s$ of area to point $M$. Thus 
\zhpar{次波源在点 $M$ 处产生的振动振幅 $\dd A$，正比于相应波前部分的面积 $\dd s$ 与该部分到点 $M$ 的距离 $r$ 之比，并且取决于波前外法线与从面元 $\dd s$ 指向点 $M$ 的直线之间的夹角 $\alpha$。于是}
\begin{equation*}%
\dd A = f\!(\alpha) \frac{a \dd s}{r},
\end{equation*}
where $a$ is a quantity proportional to the amplitude of the primary wave at points of the element $\dd s$ of area; $f\!(\alpha)$ decreases monotonically from 1 at $\alpha = 0$ to 0 at $\alpha \geqslant \pi/2$ (secondary sources do not radiate backward).\sidenote[][-1cm]{As shown by Kirchhoff,  $f\!(\alpha) \propto (1 + \cos \alpha)$, i.e. the function becomes zero only at $\alpha = \pi$; at small angles of diffraction $\alpha$, however, this more exact relation is of no significance.}
\zhpar{式中 $a$ 是与面元 $\dd s$ 处初级波振幅成正比的量；$f\!(\alpha)$ 从 $\alpha = 0$ 时的 1 单调减小，到 $\alpha \geqslant \pi/2$ 时为 0（次波源不向后辐射）。}
\zhpar{注：据基尔霍夫的结果，$f\!(\alpha) \propto (1 + \cos \alpha)$，即该函数只在 $\alpha = \pi$ 时才为零；但在衍射角 $\alpha$ 较小时，这一更精确的关系没有实际意义。}
\item If part of surface S is an opaque screen, the corresponding secondary sources (closed by the screen) do not radiate, whereas the rest radiate in the same way as in the absence of screens.\sidenote{Actually, the material of the opaque screen has an effect on the radiation of the open secondary sources that are close to the edge of the screen (at a distance of the order of the wavelength of the light).}
\zhpar{若波面 $S$ 的一部分是不透明屏，则被屏遮挡的相应次波源不辐射，其余次波源的辐射与无屏时相同。}
\zhpar{注：实际上，不透明屏的材料也会影响靠近屏边缘（距离约为光波长量级）的开放次波源的辐射。}
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{}\label{33.1.4} On the basis of the Huygens-Fresnel principle, the \redem{law of rectilinear propagation of light} in a homogeneous medium can be substantiated from the wave point of view. Assume that $S_{0}$ is a point source of monochromatic light (\figr{fig-33-03}) and $M$ is the point of observation. As the auxiliary surface $S$, a spherical
surface of radius $R$ is chosen so that the distance $L$ from point $M$ to this sphere ($L = \seg{OM}$) is of the order of $R$. Next, surface $S$ is divided into annular portions of small area, called Fresnel zones, as shown in \figr{fig-33-03}, where $\lambda$ is the wavelength of the light.
\zhpar{根据惠更斯-菲涅耳原理，可以从波动的观点论证均匀介质中光的\redem{直线传播定律}。设 $S_{0}$ 为单色光点光源（见\figr{fig-33-03}），$M$ 为观察点。取半径为 $R$ 的球面作为辅助曲面 $S$，并使点 $M$ 到该球面的距离 $L$（$L = \seg{OM}$）与 $R$ 为同一量级。然后将球面 $S$ 划分为面积很小的环带，称为菲涅耳半波带，如\figr{fig-33-03}所示，其中 $\lambda$ 为光的波长。}

\begin{figure}%[-3cm]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.8\textwidth]{figs/ch-33/fig-33-03.pdf}
\sidecaption{Establishing rectilinear propagation of light from Huygen's principle.\zhcap{由惠更斯原理确定光的直线传播。}\label{fig-33-03}}
\end{figure}

Vibrations set up at point $M$ by two adjacent zones are opposite in phase, because the path difference from like points of these zones to point $M$ is equal to $\lambda/2$. Consequently, the amplitude of the resultant vibration at point $M$ is equal to $A = A_{1} - A_{2} + A_{3} - A_{4} + \dots$ where $A_{i}$ is the amplitude of vibration set
up at point $M$ by secondary sources, located within the limits of the $i$-th zone. At £<c LlX, the areas of all the zones are the same:
\zhpar{点 $M$ 处由相邻两个半波带产生的振动相位相反，因为从这两个半波带上对应点到 $M$ 点的光程差为 $\lambda/2$。因此，点 $M$ 处合振动的振幅为 $A = A_{1} - A_{2} + A_{3} - A_{4} + \dots$，其中 $A_{i}$ 为位于第 $i$ 个半波带内的次波源在点 $M$ 处产生的振动振幅。当 $\lambda \ll R, L$ 时，所有半波带的面积相同：}
\begin{equation*}%
\sigma_{i} = \frac{\pi R L \lambda}{(R + L)}, 
\end{equation*}
and very small (at $R = L = \SI{10}{\centi\meter}$ and
$\lambda = \SI{5d-5}{\centi\meter}$ we obtain   $\sigma_{i} = \SI{8d-5}{\centi\meter\squared}$). With an increase in $i$, the distance $r_{i}$ from the zone to point $M$ also
increases, as does angle $\alpha_{i}$ between the normal to the surface of the zone and the direction of a line from the zone to point $M$.
\zhpar{且面积非常小（取 $R = L = \SI{10}{\centi\meter}$，$\lambda = \SI{5d-5}{\centi\meter}$ 时，可得 $\sigma_{i} = \SI{8d-5}{\centi\meter\squared}$）。随着 $i$ 增大，半波带到点 $M$ 的距离 $r_{i}$ 也增大，半波带法线与从该半波带指向点 $M$ 的直线之间的夹角 $\alpha_{i}$ 同样增大。}

Therefore, according to the Huygens-Fresnel principle, 
\zhpar{因此，根据惠更斯-菲涅耳原理，有如下关系：}
\begin{equation*}%
A_{1} > A_{2} > A_{3} > \dots \qand A_{i} \approx  ( A_{i-1} + A_{i+1})/2.
\end{equation*}
Consequently, the amplitude of vibration at point $M$ is equal to $A \approx A_{1}/2$ i.e. the resultant effect of the whole open wavefront is equal to one-half of the effect of the first (central) Fresnel zone, whose radius is very small. Thus, it can be assumed that practically light is propagated from $S_{0}$ to $M$ in a straight line.
\zhpar{因此，点 $M$ 处的振动振幅约为 $A \approx A_{1}/2$，即整个开放波前的总效应等于中心第一个半波带（其半径很小）效应的一半。于是，可以认为光实际上是从 $S_{0}$ 沿直线传播到 $M$ 的。}


\subsection{}\label{33.1.5} If an opaque screen, covering all, except the first, Fresnel zones for the point $M$ of observation is placed in the path of monochromatic light from source $S_{0}$, the amplitude and intensity of light at point $M$ are doubled and quadrupled, respectively, compared to their values in the absence of the screen: $A = A_{1}$
and $I = A_{1}^{2}$. Light can be intensified to a substantially greater extent by means of a \redem{zone plate}. This is a glass plate to which an opaque coating has been applied in the form of rings which enclose only the even (or only the odd) Fresnel zones, i.e. every other zone. A zone plate has the same effect on light as a converging lens.
\zhpar{若在光源 $S_{0}$ 发出的单色光路径中放置一块不透明屏，它遮挡住对观察点 $M$ 而言除第一个半波带以外的所有半波带，则与无屏时相比，点 $M$ 处的光振幅和光强分别增大为两倍和四倍：$A = A_{1}$，$I = A_{1}^{2}$。利用\redem{波带片}还可以使光得到更大程度的增强。波带片是一块玻璃片，其上镀有不透明的环形涂层，环带只覆盖偶数个（或只覆盖奇数个）菲涅耳半波带，即隔一个半波带遮挡一个。波带片对光的作用与会聚透镜相同。}

\section{Fresnel Diffraction}
\label{sec-33.2} 
\zhtitle{菲涅耳衍射}


\subsection{}\label{33.2.1} The \redem{diffraction of light} is the whole set of phenomena due lo the wave nature of light and observed in its propagation in a medium with sharply defined optical inhomogeneities (for example, in passing through apertures in opaque screens or close to the boundaries of opaque bodies). In its narrower sense, diffraction refers to the bending of light around small opaque obstacles, i.e. deviations from the laws of geometrical optics.
\zhpar{\redem{光的衍射}是指光在具有明显光学不均匀性的介质中传播时所发生的一整类现象，这些现象源于光的波动性（例如光通过不透明屏上的孔径，或靠近不透明物体的边缘）。狭义地说，衍射是指光绕过小的不透明障碍物而发生弯曲，即偏离几何光学定律的现象。}

Distinction is made between two kinds of light diffraction:
\redem{Fresnel diffraction}, or \redem{diffraction with convergent rays}, and \redem{Fraunhofer diffraction}, or \redem{diffraction with parallel rays}. In the former,
a spherical or plane wave is incident on the obstacle and the
diffraction pattern is observed on a screen at a finite distance
behind the obstacle. In the latter, a plane wave is incident on the obstacle and the diffraction pattern is observed on a screen located in the focal plane of a converging lens placed in the path of the light that passes by the obstacle. In Fresnel diffraction, a ``diffraction image'' of the obstacle is obtained on the screen; in Fraunhofer diffraction, the ``diffraction image'' is of the distant light source.
\zhpar{光的衍射分为两类：\redem{菲涅耳衍射}，又称\redem{会聚光线衍射}；以及\redem{夫琅禾费衍射}，又称\redem{平行光线衍射}。在前者中，球面波或平面波入射到障碍物上，衍射图样在障碍物后方有限距离处的屏上观察。在后者中，平面波入射到障碍物上，衍射图样则在置于经过障碍物的光路中的会聚透镜焦平面上的屏上观察。在菲涅耳衍射中，屏上得到的是障碍物的“衍射像”；而在夫琅禾费衍射中，所得“衍射像”是远处光源的像。}

\subsection{}\label{33.2.2} In the simplest Fresnel diffraction problems, the diffraction pattern can be investigated by using the method of Fresnel zones \ssect{33.1.4}.
\zhpar{在最简单的菲涅耳衍射问题中，可以利用菲涅耳半波带方法研究衍射图样（\ssect{33.1.4}）。}
\begin{marginfigure}%[-3cm]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{figs/ch-33/fig-33-04.pdf}
\sidecaption{Fresnel diffraction by a small circular aperture.\zhcap{小圆孔的菲涅耳衍射。}\label{fig-33-04}}
\end{marginfigure}

\hlblue{Example~1.} \redem{Fresnel diffraction by a small circular aperture} in an opaque screen $AB$ (\figr{fig-33-04}). When the aperture is illuminated by monochromatic light of wavelength $\lambda$, a system of alternating dark and bright interference rings is observed on screen $E$, which is parallel to $AB$. These rings have a common centre at point $O$ located opposite the centre of the aperture. If for point $O$ an even number $2k$ (where $k = 1,\, 2,\, \dots{}$) of Fresnel zones fit into the aperture, there is a dark spot at point $O$ the amplitude of light at point $O$ is less than in the absence of a screen:
\zhpar{\hlblue{例 1.}\redem{小圆孔的菲涅耳衍射}：不透明屏 $AB$ 上的一个小圆孔（见\figr{fig-33-04}）。用波长为 $\lambda$ 的单色光照明圆孔时，与 $AB$ 平行的屏 $E$ 上可观察到一系列明暗相间的干涉环。这些环具有共同的中心 $O$，该点位于圆孔中心的正对面。若圆孔内恰好容纳点 $O$ 处的偶数个（$2k$，其中 $k = 1,\, 2,\, \dots{}$）菲涅耳半波带，则点 $O$ 处为暗斑，此时点 $O$ 处的光振幅小于无屏时的振幅：}
\begin{equation*}%
A \approx \frac{1}{2} (A_{1} - A_{2k}) < \frac{A_{1}}{2},
\end{equation*}
where $A_{i}$ is the amplitude corresponding to only the ith zone. If the number of zones is odd $(2k + 1)$, there is a bright spot at point $O$ and 
\zhpar{式中 $A_{i}$ 为仅对应于第 $i$ 个半波带的振幅。若半波带数为奇数 $(2k + 1)$，则点 $O$ 处为亮斑，且}
\begin{equation*}%
A \approx \frac{1}{2} (A_{1} + A_{2k + 1}) > \frac{A_{1}}{2}.
\end{equation*}
If the aperture is illuminated by white light, a system of concentric coloured rings is observed on screen $E$.
\zhpar{若圆孔用白光照明，则屏 $E$ 上可观察到一组同心彩色环。}

The number of Fresnel zones that fit into the aperture and the
degree of contrast of the interference pattern depend upon the
ratio of the diameter $d$ of the aperture to the distance $l$ between screens $AB$ and $E$. As $d/l$ is increased, the amplitude of light at the centre of screen $E$ approaches $A_{1}/2$ and the degree of contrast of the interference rings is reduced.
\zhpar{圆孔内所容纳的菲涅耳半波带数，以及干涉图样的反差程度，取决于圆孔直径 $d$ 与屏 $AB$、$E$ 之间距离 $l$ 之比。随着 $d/l$ 增大，屏 $E$ 中心处的光振幅趋近于 $A_{1}/2$，干涉环的反差则减小。}

\subsection{}\label{33.2.3}

\hlblue{Example~2.} \redem{Fresnel diffraction by a small disk} (opaque round screen). The method of constructing open Fresnel zones on the wavefront $S$ of the incident monochromatic spherical wave is illustrated in \figr{fig-33-05}. The interference pattern on screen $E$ consists of alternating bright and dark rings with their centre at point $O$, where the interference maximum (\redem{Poisson's spot}) is always observed. The amplitude of light at point $O$ is one-half of the amplitude Alt corresponding to the effect at this point of only the single first open Fresnel zone: $A = A_{1}/2$.
\zhpar{\hlblue{例 2.}\redem{小圆盘的菲涅耳衍射}（不透明圆屏）。入射单色球面波的波前 $S$ 上构造开放菲涅耳半波带的方法如\figr{fig-33-05}所示。屏 $E$ 上的干涉图样由以点 $O$ 为中心、明暗相间的环组成，点 $O$ 处总是出现干涉极大（\redem{泊松亮斑}）。点 $O$ 处的光振幅为仅对应于该点上第一个开放半波带的振幅 $A_{1}$ 的一半：$A = A_{1}/2$。}


When the disk is illuminated by white light, a white spot is
observed at the centre of the screen. This spot is surrounded by a system of concentric coloured rings. As the ratio of the diameter $d$ of the disk to its distance $l$ from screen $E$ is increased, the brightness of Poisson's spot is gradually reduced and the next dark ring becomes broader, forming a region of shadow beyond the disk.
\zhpar{用白光照明圆盘时，屏的中心出现一个白色亮斑，其周围为一组同心彩色环。随着圆盘直径 $d$ 与其到屏 $E$ 的距离 $l$ 之比增大，泊松亮斑的亮度逐渐减弱，下一个暗环变宽，并在圆盘后方形成阴影区域。}

\label{sec-33.3}

\subsection{}\label{33.3.1} 

\hlblue{Example~1.} \redem{Diffraction of light by a narrow long slit} in an opaque screen (\figr{fig-33-06}). The slit width $\seg{BC} - b$, and its length in the direction perpendicular to the plane of the figure is $l \gg b$.
Light is normally incident on the surface of the slit so that vibrations at all points of the slit are in phase.
\zhpar{\hlblue{例 1.}\redem{光通过窄长狭缝的衍射}：不透明屏上的狭缝（见\figr{fig-33-06}）。狭缝宽度为 $b = \seg{BC}$，其沿垂直于图面方向的长度为 $l \gg b$。光垂直入射到狭缝表面，因此狭缝上各点的振动相位相同。}

The diffraction pattern is observed on screen $E$, which is placed in the focal plane of converging lens $L$. Parallel
rays $BM$ and $CN$, travelling from the edges of the slit at the \redem{diffraction angle} $\psi$) to the direction of incident light, are collected by the lens at its secondary focus $F_{\psi}$. The lens are made so that the paths of the rays of light $BMF_{\psi}$ and $DNF_{\psi}$ , where $D$ is the foot of the perpendicular dropped from point $B$ to ray $CN$, are tautochronous paths \ssect{32.3.2}. Therefore, the result of the interference at point $F_{\psi}$of the screen depends upon the path difference $\seg{CD} = b \sin \psi$ and the wavelength $\lambda$ of the light.
\zhpar{衍射图样在位于会聚透镜 $L$ 焦平面上的屏 $E$ 上观察。从狭缝两边缘以衍射角 $\psi$（相对于入射光方向）出发的平行光线 $BM$ 与 $CN$，经透镜会聚于其副焦点 $F_{\psi}$。透镜的设计使光线路径 $BMF_{\psi}$ 与 $DNF_{\psi}$ 为等光程路径（\ssect{32.3.2}），其中 $D$ 为从点 $B$ 向光线 $CN$ 所作垂线的垂足。因此，屏上点 $F_{\psi}$ 处的干涉结果取决于光程差 $\seg{CD} = b \sin \psi$ 以及光的波长 $\lambda$。}
\begin{enumerate}[label = (\alph*)]
\item \redem{Approximate solution.} The slit can be divided in width into Fresnel zones, having the form of strips parallel to edge $B$, such that the path difference from the edges of the strips is equal to $\lambda /2$. The number of such Fresnel zones that fit into the slit is equal to $2 b \abs{\sin \psi} \lambda$ the zones radiate light entirely equally in the direction being considered; and the vibrations set up at point by two adjacent zones are equal in amplitude and opposite in phase. Therefore, when there is an even number of zones:
\zhpar{\redem{近似解}。狭缝可以沿宽度方向划分为平行于边缘 $B$ 的条带形菲涅耳半波带，使相邻条带边缘之间的光程差为 $\lambda /2$。能够容纳于狭缝中的此类菲涅耳半波带数为 $2 b \abs{\sin \psi} \lambda$。各半波带沿所考虑方向辐射的光完全相同，相邻两个半波带在该点处产生的振动振幅相等、相位相反。因此，当半波带数为偶数时：}
\begin{equation*}%
b \sin \psi = \pm 2 m \frac{\lambda}{2},
\end{equation*}
 where $m = 1,\,2, \, 3 \dots{}$ and a diffraction minimum is observed (complete darkness). When the number of zones is odd:
\zhpar{式中 $m = 1,\,2,\, 3 \dots{}$，此时观察到衍射极小（完全暗纹）。当半波带数为奇数时：}
\begin{equation*}%
b \sin \psi = \pm (2 m + 1 ) \frac{\lambda}{2},
\end{equation*}
 where $m = 1,\,2, \, 3 \dots{}$ and a diffraction maximum corresponding to one Fresnel zone, is observed. The brightest central maximum is observed at the principal focus $F_{0}$ of the lens $(\psi = 0)$. With an increase in $m$ the width of the Fresnel zones and the intensity of the maxima are rapidly reduced.
\zhpar{式中 $m = 1,\,2,\, 3 \dots{}$，此时观察到对应于一个菲涅耳半波带的衍射极大。最亮的中央极大位于透镜的主焦点 $F_{0}$（$\psi = 0$）处。随着 $m$ 增大，菲涅耳半波带的宽度与极大的强度迅速减小。}
\item \redem{Exact solution.} The slit is divided into a very great number of identical very narrow strips, parallel to edge $B$. Secondary waves radiated by these elements of the slit, set up vibrations at point $F_{\psi}$, that have the same small amplitudes, and their initial phases continuously fill an interval of width $\Delta \varphi = (2\pi b \sin \psi)/\lambda$. According to \ssect{32.4.4}, the amplitude and
intensity of light at point $F_{psi}$ equal:
\zhpar{\redem{精确解}。将狭缝划分为极多的相同且极窄的、平行于边缘 $B$ 的条带。这些条带辐射的次波在点 $F_{\psi}$ 处产生振幅相同且很小的振动，它们的初相位连续地充满宽度为 $\Delta \varphi = (2\pi b \sin \psi)/\lambda$ 的区间。根据\ssect{32.4.4}，点 $F_{\psi}$ 处光的振幅与光强为：}
\begin{equation*}
A_{\psi} = A_{0} \abs{\frac{\sin \dfrac{\pi b \sin \psi}{\lambda}}{\dfrac{\pi b \sin \psi}{\lambda}}}, \qand  I_{\psi} = I_{0} \frac{\sin^{2} \dfrac{\pi b \sin \psi}{\lambda}}{\left(\dfrac{\pi b \sin \psi}{\lambda}\right)^{2}},
\end{equation*}
where $A_{0}$ and $I_{0}$ are the amplitude and intensity at the central maximum $(\psi = 0)$. 
\zhpar{式中 $A_{0}$ 与 $I_{0}$ 分别为中央极大（$\psi = 0$）处的振幅与光强。}

The condition for diffraction minima of light is the same as in
the approximate solution: 
\zhpar{光衍射极小的条件与近似解中的相同：}
\begin{equation*}%
b \sin \psi = \pm 2 m \frac{\lambda}{2},
\end{equation*}
 where $m = 1,\,2, \, 3 \dots{}$
The condition for diffraction maxima is of the form
\zhpar{式中 $m = 1,\,2,\, 3 \dots{}$。衍射极大的条件具有如下形式：}
\begin{equation*}%
\tan \frac{\pi b \sin \psi}{\lambda} = \frac{\pi b \sin \psi}{\lambda},
\end{equation*} 
and it differs only slightly from the condition obtained by means of the Fresnel zones method.
\zhpar{它与用菲涅耳半波带方法得到的条件仅略有不同。}
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{}\label{33.3.2} If white light, instead of monochromatic light, falls on the slit, the central maximum is white with iridescent colouring along its edges. All the other interference fringes are coloured because different angles $\psi$ and different points $F_{\psi}$ on the screen correspond to both minima and maxima of the same orders $m$,  depending upon the wavelength $\lambda$.
\zhpar{若用白光代替单色光照射狭缝，则中央极大为白色，其边缘带有彩色虹彩。其他所有干涉条纹均为彩色，因为同一级次 $m$ 的极小与极大，依波长 $\lambda$ 的不同，分别对应于屏上不同的衍射角 $\psi$ 与不同的点 $F_{\psi}$。}

As the width $b$ of the slit is reduced, the width of the central maximum is increased because of the increase of angles   
\zhpar{当狭缝宽度 $b$ 减小时，中央极大的宽度增大，这是由于下式所示的角度增大：}
\begin{equation*}%
\psi_{1} = \pm \arcsin \frac{\lambda}{b},
\end{equation*} 
which correspond to minima of the first order, limiting the central maximum. At $b \leqslant \lambda$ the illumination of the screen is reduced monotonically from the centre (point $F_{0}$) toward the edges. When the slit is very wide ($b \gg \lambda$), a bright and sharp image of Ithe light source, formed by lens $L$ according to the laws of geometrical optics, is seen on the screen.
\zhpar{这些角度对应于限制中央极大的一级极小。当 $b \leqslant \lambda$ 时，屏上的照度从中心（点 $F_{0}$）向边缘单调减小。当狭缝很宽（$b \gg \lambda$）时，屏上可以看到由透镜 $L$ 依据几何光学定律所成的光源明亮而清晰的像。}


\subsection{}\label{33.3.3} \hlblue{Example~2.} \redem{Diffraction of light by a circular aperture}.
A plane monochromatic light wave is normally incident on the
aperture, i.e. perpendicular to the plane of the aperture. The
diffraction pattern is observed in the focal plane of a converging lens arranged beyond the aperture so that its optical axis is perpendicular to the plane of the aperture. The diffraction pattern has the form of a bright white spot at the principal focus $F_{0}$ of the lens and, concentric about this spot, alternating dark and bright rings. The intensity of the bright rings is very low compared to that $I_{0}$ of the central maximum, and decreases with an increase in their radius. For instance, the intensity of the nearest maximum of the first order to the central one is $I_{1} < 0.02 I_{0}$.
\zhpar{\hlblue{例 2.}\redem{光通过圆孔的衍射}。平面单色光波垂直入射到圆孔上，即垂直于圆孔所在平面入射。衍射图样在圆孔后方放置的会聚透镜焦平面上观察，该透镜的光轴与圆孔平面垂直。衍射图样呈现为位于透镜主焦点 $F_{0}$ 处的明亮白色光斑，以及围绕该光斑的一组明暗相间的同心环。与中央极大的光强 $I_{0}$ 相比，亮环的光强很低，并且随半径增大而减小。例如，与中央极大相邻的一级极大的光强为 $I_{1} < 0.02 I_{0}$。}

The diffraction angle $\psi_{1}$ corresponding to the first dark ring, bounding the central maximum, complies with the condition 
\zhpar{界定中央极大的第一暗环所对应的衍射角 $\psi_{1}$ 满足下列条件：}
\begin{equation*}%
\sin \psi_{1} = 1.22 \frac{\lambda}{D},
\end{equation*}
where $D$ is the diameter of the aperture and $\lambda$ is the wavelength of the light.
\zhpar{式中 $D$ 为圆孔的直径，$\lambda$ 为光的波长。}

If light falls on the aperture at a small angle $\alpha$ to the normal to the plane of the aperture, the interference pattern remains practically unchanged, but its centre is displaced to a secondary focus of the lens, corresponding to the angle  $\psi = \alpha$.
\zhpar{若光以很小的角度 $\alpha$ 入射到圆孔平面上，则干涉图样基本保持不变，但其中心移至透镜的副焦点，该副焦点对应于衍射角 $\psi = \alpha$。}

\subsection{}\label{33.3.4} \hlblue{Example~3.} \redem{Diffraction of light by a one-dimensional
grating}. A one-dimensfbnal \redem{diffraction grating} is a system of large number $N$ of parallel slits in a screen, the slits being identical in width and separated by opaque strips, also of equal width. Shown in \figr{fig-33-07} are only two adjacent slits of the grating. The quantity $d = a + b$, where $a = \seg{CD}$ is the width of an opaque strip between two slits
and $b = \seg{BC}$ is the slit width, is called the \redem{diffraction grating constant}, or \redem{interval}.
\zhpar{\hlblue{例 3.}\redem{一维光栅的衍射}。\redem{一维衍射光栅}是屏上由大量（$N$ 个）平行狭缝组成的系统，各狭缝宽度相同，并由同样宽度的不透明条带隔开。如\figr{fig-33-07}所示，仅画出了光栅上相邻的两条狭缝。其中 $a = \seg{CD}$ 为两狭缝之间不透明条带的宽度，$b = \seg{BC}$ 为狭缝宽度，$d = a + b$ 称为\redem{衍射光栅常数}，又称\redem{间距}。}

In analysing the diffraction pattern on screen $E$, mounted in
the focal plane of converging lens $L$, it is necessary to take into account the interference of the secondary waves not only from different portions of one slit, but from the different slits as well. If a plane monochromatic wave is normally incident on the grating, the vibrations at all points of the slits are in phase.
\zhpar{在分析位于会聚透镜 $L$ 焦平面上的屏 $E$ 上的衍射图样时，必须同时考虑来自同一狭缝不同部分的次波的干涉，以及来自不同狭缝的次波的干涉。若平面单色波垂直入射到光栅上，则各狭缝上所有点的振动相位相同。}

Vibrations set up at the arbitrary point of the focal plane
of lens $L$ by each of the slits coincide in amplitude $(A_{1})$ and differ in phase. For each pair of adjacent slits the phase difference $\Delta \varphi_{0}$ between these vibrations is the same. It depends upon the wavelength $\lambda$ and the path difference from like points of the
slits (for instance, points $B$ and $D$), i.e. on the length $\seg{KD} = d \sin \psi$, where $K$ is the foot of the perpendicular, dropped from point $B$ to the ray $DN$. Thus
\zhpar{各狭缝在透镜 $L$ 焦平面上任一点处产生的振动振幅（$A_{1}$）相同，但相位不同。对于每一对相邻狭缝，这些振动之间的相位差 $\Delta \varphi_{0}$ 都相同。该相位差取决于波长 $\lambda$，以及相应点（例如点 $B$ 与点 $D$）之间的光程差，即取决于长度 $\seg{KD} = d \sin \psi$，其中 $K$ 为从点 $B$ 向光线 $DN$ 所作垂线的垂足。于是}
\begin{equation*}%
\Delta \varphi_{0} = \frac{2 \pi d}{\lambda} \sin \psi.
\end{equation*}
Therefore, according to sections \ssect{32.4.2} and \ssect{33.3.1}, the amplitude and intensity of the resultant vibration at point $F_{\psi}$ equal 
\zhpar{因此，根据\ssect{32.4.2}与\ssect{33.3.1}，点 $F_{\psi}$ 处合振动的振幅与光强为：}
\begin{align*}
A &= A_{0} \abs{\frac{\sin \dfrac{\pi b \sin \psi}{\lambda}}{\dfrac{\pi b \sin \psi}{\lambda}} \cdot \frac{\sin \dfrac{\pi N \sin \psi}{\lambda}}{\sin \dfrac{\pi d \sin \psi}{\lambda}}}, \qand \\  
I &= I_{0} \frac{\sin^{2} \dfrac{\pi b \sin \psi}{\lambda}}{\left(\dfrac{\pi b \sin \psi}{\lambda}\right)^{2}} \cdot \frac{\sin^{2} \dfrac{\pi N \sin \psi}{\lambda}}{\sin^{2} \dfrac{\pi d \sin \psi}{\lambda}},
\end{align*}
where $A_{0}$ and $I_{0}$ are the amplitude and intensity of the vibration at point $F_{0}$ (i.e. at $\psi = 0$) due to the effect of a \redem{single} slit.
\zhpar{式中 $A_{0}$ 与 $I_{0}$ 分别为仅由\redem{单条}狭缝引起的、点 $F_{0}$（即 $\psi = 0$）处的振动振幅与光强。}

\subsection{}\label{33.3.5} The \redem{principal minima} in the diffraction of light by a diffraction grating occur at diffraction angles $\psi$, corresponding to interference minima in diffraction by a single slit \ssect{33.3.1}.
Thus
\zhpar{衍射光栅产生光衍射时的\redem{主极小}，出现在与单缝衍射中干涉极小相对应的衍射角 $\psi$ 处（\ssect{33.3.1}）。即}
\begin{equation*}%
b \sin \psi = \pm m\lambda,
\end{equation*}
where $m = 1,\,2, \, 3 \dots{}$.
\zhpar{式中 $m = 1,\,2,\, 3 \dots{}$。}

In these directions none of the slits provide light (``each slit extinguishes itself'').
\zhpar{在这些方向上，所有狭缝都不提供光（“每条狭缝都自行消光”）。}

The \redem{principal maxima} \ssect{32.4.3} correspond to diffraction angles $\psi$ that comply with the condition
\zhpar{\redem{主极大}（\ssect{32.4.3}）对应于满足下列条件的衍射角 $\psi$：}
\begin{equation*}%
d \sin \psi = \pm n \lambda,
\end{equation*}
where $n = 1,\,2, \, 3 \dots{}$ is the \redem{order of the principal maximum}.
\zhpar{式中 $n = 1,\,2,\, 3 \dots{}$ 称为\redem{主极大的级次}。}

\hlblue{Note.} If certain values of $\psi$ simultaneously comply with the conditions for both principal maxima and principal minima, then the principal maxima corresponding to these values of $\psi$ are not observed. For example, if $d = 2b$, then all the even principal maxima ($n = 2, \,4, \,6, $ etc.) are absent.
The intensity of a principal maximum of the $n$-th order is
\zhpar{\hlblue{注：}若某些 $\psi$ 值同时满足主极大与主极小的条件，则与这些 $\psi$ 值对应的主极大不被观察到。例如，若 $d = 2b$，则所有偶数级主极大（$n = 2, \,4, \,6, $ 等）都不出现。$n$ 级主极大的光强为：}
\begin{equation*}%
I_{n} = I_{0} \left( \frac{Nd}{\pi n b} \right)^{2} \sin^{2} \frac{\pi n b}{d}.
\end{equation*}

\subsection{}\label{33.3.6} Between each two principal maxima, there are $N - 1$ \redem{additional minima} that comply with the condition
\zhpar{在每两个相邻主极大之间，有 $N - 1$ 个\redem{附加极小}满足下列条件：}
\begin{equation*}%
d \sin \psi = \pm \frac{p \lambda}{N},
\end{equation*}
where p may be any positive whole number with the exception
of $N$, $2N$, $3N$, etc. Accordingly, there are $N-2$ additional maxima whose intensity is negligibly low compared to that of the principal maxima.
\zhpar{式中 $p$ 可以取除 $N$、$2N$、$3N$ 等以外的任何正整数。相应地，还有 $N-2$ 个附加极大，其强度与主极大相比极低，可以忽略。}

The \redem{angular ``width'' of a principal maximum} of the $n$-th order, i.e. the difference between the values of the angles  $\psi$ corresponding  to the additional minima bounding this maximum, is 
\zhpar{\redem{第 $n$ 级主极大的角“宽度”}，即界定该极大的附加极小所对应的角 $\psi$ 之差，为：}
\begin{equation*}%
\Delta \psi_{n} = \frac{2 \lambda}{N d \cos \psi_{n}} = \frac{2 \lambda}{L \cos \psi_{n}},
\end{equation*}
where $\psi_{n} = \arcsin (n\lambda /d)$, and $L = Nd$ is the length of the diffraction grating. For principal maxima of not particularly high orders, angles $\psi_{n}$ are small and $\cos \psi_{n} \approx 1$, so that $\Delta \psi_{n} \approx 2 \lambda/L$.
\zhpar{式中 $\psi_{n} = \arcsin (n\lambda /d)$，$L = Nd$ 为衍射光栅的长度。对于级次不太高的主极大，角 $\psi_{n}$ 很小，$\cos \psi_{n} \approx 1$，因此 $\Delta \psi_{n} \approx 2 \lambda/L$。}


\subsection{}\label{33.3.7} With monochromatic light the diffraction pattern on screen $E$ in the focal plane of lens $L$ (\figr{fig-33-07}) has, at large $N$ values, the form of narrow and bright principal maxima separated by practically dark and wide intervals. If the ratio $d/b$ is an irrational number, the intensities of the principal maxima \ssect{33.3.5} decrease monotonically with an increase in the order $n$ of the maximum.
\zhpar{对于单色光，当 $N$ 很大时，透镜 $L$ 焦平面上屏 $E$ 的衍射图样（见\figr{fig-33-07}）呈现为狭窄明亮的主极大，主极大之间被近乎暗的宽间隔隔开。若比值 $d/b$ 为无理数，则主极大的强度（\ssect{33.3.5}）随级次 $n$ 增大而单调减小。}

When the grating is illuminated by white light, an uncoloured
central zero-order maximum is observed on the screen. On both
sides of this maximum, \redem{diffraction spectra} of the 1st, 2nd, etc. orders are observed. These spectra appear as iridescent bands in which there is a continuous transition in colour from blue-violet at the inner edge of the spectrum to red at the outer edge.
\zhpar{用白光照明光栅时，屏上观察到无色的零级中央极大。在其两侧，观察到一级、二级等衍射光谱。这些光谱呈现为彩色条带，从内边缘的蓝紫色到外边缘的红色，颜色连续过渡。}

\sidecaption{Diffraction of light with inclined incidence on a grating.\zhcap{光以倾斜方式入射到光栅上时的衍射。}\label{fig-33-08}}

\subsection{}\label{33.3.8} Upon inclined incidence of light on a diffraction grating (\figr{fig-33-08}), the condition for the principal maxima is of the form
\zhpar{当光以倾斜方式入射到衍射光栅上时（见\figr{fig-33-08}），主极大的条件具有如下形式：}
\begin{equation*}%
d (\sin \psi - \sin i) = \pm n \lambda,
\end{equation*}
where $n = 1,\,2, \, 3 \dots{}$, $i$ is the angle of incidence of light on the surface of the grating. Frequently, the directions of the rays of light falling on the grating and diffracted from it are specified by the angles $\alpha_{0}$ and $\alpha$ which these rays make with the axis $OX$ of coordinates, arranged in the plane of the grating, perpendicular to the slits. In this case, the condition for the principal maxima can be rewritten in the form
\zhpar{式中 $n = 1,\,2,\, 3 \dots{}$，$i$ 为光在光栅表面上的入射角。通常，入射到光栅上的光线与经光栅衍射后的光线的方向，由它们与位于光栅平面内、垂直于狭缝的坐标轴 $OX$ 所成的角 $\alpha_{0}$ 与 $\alpha$ 给出。在这种情况下，主极大的条件可改写为如下形式：}
\begin{equation*}%
d (\cos \alpha - \cos \alpha_{0}) = \pm n \lambda.
\end{equation*}

\subsection{}\label{33.3.9} Two screens are said to be \redem{complementary} if the apertures in one of them exactly coincide in shape, size and relative position with the opaque areas in the other screen, and vice versa. Complementary, for example, are an opaque screen in the form of a circle (disk) of radius $R$ and an opaque screen having a circular aperture of the same radius $R$. On the basis of the Huygens-Fresnel principle, it is possible to prove \redem{Babinets theorem} (also called \redem{Babinet's principle}). This theorem states
that in Fraunhofer diffraction by any screen, the intensity of
diffracted light in any direction, except that of the propagation of a plane wave falling on the screen, should be the same as in diffraction by the complementary screen.
\zhpar{若两个屏中，一个屏上的孔径在形状、大小和相对位置上恰好与另一个屏上的不透明区域相同，反之亦然，则称这两个屏为\redem{互补屏}。例如，半径为 $R$ 的不透明圆屏（圆盘）与具有相同半径 $R$ 的圆孔的不透明屏互为互补屏。依据惠更斯-菲涅耳原理，可以证明\redem{巴比涅定理}（又称\redem{巴比涅原理}）。该定理指出：对于任意屏的夫琅禾费衍射，除入射平面波在屏上的传播方向外，其他任意方向上衍射光的强度，都与互补屏衍射时的强度相同。}


\subsection{}\label{33.3.10} \hlblue{Example~4.} \redem{Diffraction by a large number of identical and identically oriented obstacles}. The light intensity $I$ at an
arbitrary point $M$ of the diffraction pattern, like in diffraction by a one-dimensional grating \ssect{33.3.4}, can be presented in the form: 
\zhpar{\hlblue{例 4.}\redem{大量相同且取向相同的障碍物的衍射}。与一维衍射光栅的情形（\ssect{33.3.4}）类似，衍射图样中任意点 $M$ 处的光强 $I$ 可以写成如下形式：}
\begin{equation*}%
I = f I_{1}. 
\end{equation*}
Here $I_{1}$ is the intensity at point $M$ upon diffraction
of the same incident plane wave by a \redem{single} obstacle. The function $f$ depends only upon the number and relative arrangement of the obstacles.
\zhpar{式中 $I_{1}$ 为相同入射平面波被\redem{单个}障碍物衍射时，点 $M$ 处的光强。函数 $f$ 仅取决于障碍物的数目及其相对排列。}

If the obstacles are arranged quite randomly with respect to one another and their number $N$ is large, then $f \approx N$. In this case, the distribution of light intensity is the same as in diffraction by a single obstacle. But the intensity at each point of the diffraction pattern is $N$ times as much. For example, in diffraction of light by a glass plate coated with spherical dust particles of diameter $d$, a system of bright concentric interference rings is observed. The sizes of these rings correspond to the diffraction of light by an opaque disk of diameter $d$ or, in accordance with Babinet's principle \ssect{33.3.9}, by a round aperture of the same diameter.
\zhpar{若障碍物之间为随机排列，且其数目 $N$ 很大，则 $f \approx N$。在这种情形下，光强的分布与单个障碍物衍射时相同，但衍射图样中每一点的光强为其 $N$ 倍。例如，当光通过一块涂有直径为 $d$ 的球形尘埃颗粒的玻璃片时，可观察到一组明亮的同心干涉环。这些环的大小对应于直径为 $d$ 的不透明圆盘的衍射，或者根据巴比涅原理（\ssect{33.3.9}），对应于同样直径的圆孔的衍射。}

\section{Diffraction by a Space Lattice}
\label{sec-33.4} 
\zhtitle{空间点阵的衍射}


\subsection{}\label{33.4.1} \redem{A space, or three-dimensional, difraction grating} is an optically nonhomogeneous medium in which the inhomogeneities are periodically repeated in varying all three spatial coordinates. An example of a space diffraction grating is the crystal lattice of a crystalline solid. Particles at the lattice points (atoms, molecules or ions) serve as orderly arranged centres that coherently scatter light falling on them. Assume that $d_{1}$, $d_{2}$ and $d_{3}$ are the lattice constants along the three coordinate axes $\xi  \, \eta $ and $\zeta$ that are oriented along three edges of the lattice intersecting in one of its points. Then in Fraunhofer diffraction \ssect{33.2.1}, the principal maxima comply with the \redem{Laue conditions}
\zhpar{\redem{空间（三维）衍射光栅}是一种光学上不均匀的介质，其不均匀性在三个空间坐标方向上都呈周期性重复。固体的晶格就是空间衍射光栅的一个例子。位于格点上的粒子（原子、分子或离子）作为有序排列的中心，对入射到其上的光进行相干散射。设 $d_{1}$、$d_{2}$ 和 $d_{3}$ 分别为沿三条坐标轴 $\xi  \, \eta $ 和 $\zeta$ 方向的晶格常数，这三条坐标轴沿交于晶格某一格点的三条棱边取向。那么，在夫琅禾费衍射（\ssect{33.2.1}）中，主极大满足\redem{劳厄条件}：}
\begin{align*}% 
d_{1} (\cos \alpha - \cos \alpha_{0}) & = n_{1} \lambda,\\
d_{2} (\cos \beta - \cos \beta_{0}) & = n_{2} \lambda, \qand \\
d_{3} (\cos \gamma - \cos \gamma_{0}) & = n_{3} \lambda .
\end{align*}
Here $\alpha_{0}$, $\beta_{0}$ and $\gamma_{0}$ and $\alpha$, $\beta$ and $\gamma$ are the angles between the coordinate
axes $\xi  \, \eta $ and $\zeta$, and the directions of propagation of the incident and diffracted rays, respectively, $n_{1}$, $n_{2}$ and $n_{3}$ are whole numbers determining the order of the maximum, and $\lambda$ is the wavelength of the light. The Laue conditions follow from the relations \ssect{33.3.8} for diffraction maxima in inclined incidence of
light on a one-dimensional diffraction grating.
\zhpar{式中 $\alpha_{0}$、$\beta_{0}$ 和 $\gamma_{0}$ 分别为入射光线传播方向与坐标轴 $\xi  \, \eta $ 和 $\zeta$ 的夹角，$\alpha$、$\beta$ 和 $\gamma$ 分别为衍射光线传播方向与各坐标轴的夹角；$n_{1}$、$n_{2}$ 和 $n_{3}$ 为决定极大级次的整数，$\lambda$ 为光的波长。劳厄条件可由一维衍射光栅斜入射时衍射极大的关系（\ssect{33.3.8}）得出。}

\clearpage

\subsection{}\label{33.4.2} Of the three angles $\alpha$, $\beta$ and $\gamma$ (and likewise for angles
$\alpha_{0}$, $\beta_{0}$ and $\gamma_{0}$) only two angles are independent because they must comply with one geometric relationship whose specific form depends upon the angles between the coordinate axes $\xi  \, \eta $ and $\zeta$. For example, if the coordinate axes are mutually per­ pendicular, i.e. if the lattice is orthogonal, the geometric relationship
between $\alpha$, $\beta$ and $\gamma$ is of the form:
\zhpar{在 $\alpha$、$\beta$ 和 $\gamma$ 这三个角（$\alpha_{0}$、$\beta_{0}$、$\gamma_{0}$ 也同样）中，只有两个角是独立的，因为它们必须满足一个几何关系，该关系的具体形式取决于坐标轴 $\xi  \, \eta $ 和 $\zeta$ 之间的夹角。例如，若坐标轴两两互相垂直，即晶格为正交晶格，则 $\alpha$、$\beta$ 和 $\gamma$ 之间的几何关系为如下形式：}
\begin{equation*}%
cos^{2} \alpha + cos^{2} \beta + \cos^{2} \gamma = 1.
\end{equation*}
Therefore, with an arbitrarily given direction of incidence on
a space lattice of monochromatic light with a given wavelength $\lambda$, it is impossible, in general, to find values of $\alpha$, $\beta$ and $\gamma$ that simultaneously comply with the geometric relationship and Laue's three conditions. The only exception is the zero-order maximum $(n_{1} = n_{2} = n_{3} = 0)$, for which $\alpha = \alpha_{0}$, $\beta = \beta_{0}$ and $\gamma = \gamma_{0}$.
\zhpar{因此，对于给定波长 $\lambda$ 的单色光以任意给定方向入射到空间点阵上，一般不可能找到同时满足该几何关系和劳厄三个条件的 $\alpha$、$\beta$ 和 $\gamma$ 值。唯一的例外是零级极大（$n_{1} = n_{2} = n_{3} = 0$），此时 $\alpha = \alpha_{0}$、$\beta = \beta_{0}$ 和 $\gamma = \gamma_{0}$。}

In order to observe a diffraction maximum of the order ($n_{1}$, $n_{2}$ and $n_{3}$) at given values of the angles $\alpha_{0}$, $\beta_{0}$ and $\gamma_{0}$, it is necessary for the incident light to have a certain definite wavelength.
For instance, in the case of an orthogonal lattice, the wavelength should be
\zhpar{要在给定的 $\alpha_{0}$、$\beta_{0}$ 和 $\gamma_{0}$ 值下观察到级次为（$n_{1}$、$n_{2}$ 和 $n_{3}$）的衍射极大，入射光必须具有确定的波长。例如，对于正交晶格，波长应为：}
\begin{equation*}%
\lambda = -2 \frac{\dfrac{n_{1}}{d_{1}} \cos \alpha_{0} + \dfrac{n_{2}}{d_{2}} \cos \beta_{0} + \dfrac{n_{3}}{d_{3}} \cos \gamma_{0} }{\left( \dfrac{n_{1}}{d_{1}}\right)^{2} + \left( \dfrac{n_{2}}{d_{2}}\right)^{2} + \left( \dfrac{n_{3}}{d_{3}}\right)^{2}}
\end{equation*}
If the wavelength $\lambda$ of the incident light is a fixed value, the Laue conditions and the geometric relationship between angles $\alpha$, $\beta$ and $\gamma$ can simultaneously be complied with by a suitable choice of the direction of light incidence on the diffraction
grating, i.e. of angles $\alpha_{0}$, $\beta_{0}$ and $\gamma_{0}$.
\zhpar{若入射光的波长 $\lambda$ 为固定值，则通过适当选择光的入射方向，即选择角 $\alpha_{0}$、$\beta_{0}$ 和 $\gamma_{0}$，可以使劳厄条件与角 $\alpha$、$\beta$ 和 $\gamma$ 之间的几何关系同时得到满足。}

\subsection{}\label{33.4.3} It follows from Laue's conditions that at $\lambda \geqslant 2d_{max}$, where $d_{max}$ is the maximum of the values of $d_{1}$, $d_{2}$ and $d_{3}$, there should be no maxima, except the zero-order one $(n_{1} = n_{2} = n_{3} = 0)$. Light of such wavelengths propagates in a medium ``without noticing'' its inhomogeneities, i.e. without undergoing diffraction. Hence, the condition $\lambda \geqslant 2d_{max}$ is called the \redem{condition for optical homogeneity of the medium}.
\zhpar{由劳厄条件可知，当 $\lambda \geqslant 2d_{max}$ 时（其中 $d_{max}$ 为 $d_{1}$、$d_{2}$ 和 $d_{3}$ 中的最大值），除零级极大（$n_{1} = n_{2} = n_{3} = 0$）外不应出现任何极大。这种波长的光在介质中传播时“察觉不到”介质的不均匀性，即不发生衍射。因此，条件 $\lambda \geqslant 2d_{max}$ 称为\redem{介质光学均匀性的条件}。}

The crystal lattice constants of solids are considerably shorter than the wavelengths of visible light ($d_{i} \approx   \SI{5d-7}{\meter}$, whereas $\lambda_{vis} \approx \SI{5d-7}{\meter}$). Hence, crystals are optically homogeneous
media for visible light.\sidenote{Molecular scattering of visible light \ssect{34.3.1} is possible in crystals. It is associated with the impairment of their optical homogeneity due to fluctuations in density.} At the same time, crystals are natural diffraction lattices for $X$ rays \ssect{31.4.4}.
\zhpar{固体的晶格常数远小于可见光的波长（$d_{i} \approx \SI{5d-7}{\meter}$，而 $\lambda_{vis} \approx \SI{5d-7}{\meter}$），因此对于可见光而言，晶体是光学均匀介质。与此同时，晶体是 $X$ 射线的天然衍射光栅（\ssect{31.4.4}）。}
\zhpar{注：晶体中可以发生可见光的分子散射（\ssect{34.3.1}），这与密度涨落引起的晶体光学均匀性受损有关。}

\subsection{}\label{33.4.4} The diffraction of $X$ rays by crystals can be interpreted as the result of interference of $X$ rays that are mirror reflected from a system of parallel planes passing through the crystal lattice points. They are called \redem{planes of atoms}, or \redem{lattice planes, of the crystal}. The distance d between two adjacent lattice planes is railed the \redem{interplanar spacing}, or \redem{distance}, and the angle $\theta$ between the incident ray and the lattice plane (\figr{fig-33-09}) is called
the \redem{grazing angle}. The path difference of rays reflected from two adjacent lattice planes\sidenote{$X$ rays are not refracted in crystals because the values of the
refractive index \ssect{31.5.1} of all crystals for electromagnetic radiation of such high frequency are practically equal to unity.} is $\Delta = \seg{BC} + \seg{BD} = 2d \sin \theta$. Therefore, according to \ssect{32.2.1}, reflection is observed only in the directions, corresponding to diffraction maxima that comply with the Wulff-Bragg condition:
\zhpar{晶体对 $X$ 射线的衍射，可以解释为从一组通过晶格格点的平行晶面镜面反射的 $X$ 射线相互干涉的结果。这些平面称为\redem{原子面}，或\redem{晶体的晶格面}。相邻两晶格面之间的距离 $d$ 称为\redem{面间距}，入射光线与晶格面之间的夹角 $\theta$（见\figr{fig-33-09}）称为\redem{掠射角}。从相邻两晶格面反射的光线的光程差为 $\Delta = \seg{BC} + \seg{BD} = 2d \sin \theta$。因此，根据\ssect{32.2.1}，只在与满足乌尔夫-布拉格条件的衍射极大相对应的方向上才观察到反射：}
\zhpar{注：$X$ 射线在晶体中不发生折射，因为所有晶体对于如此高频的电磁辐射的折射率（\ssect{31.5.1}）实际上都等于 1。}
\begin{equation*}%
2d \sin \theta = m \lambda,
\end{equation*}
where $m = 1, \,2,\, \dots{}$ is the order of the diffraction maximum.
\zhpar{式中 $m = 1, \,2,\, \dots{}$ 为衍射极大的级次。}
\begin{marginfigure}%[-3cm]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.8\textwidth]{figs/ch-33/fig-33-09.pdf}
\sidecaption{$X$ ray diffraction.\zhcap{$X$ 射线衍射。}\label{fig-33-09}}
\end{marginfigure}

\subsection{}\label{33.4.5} The experimental method of investigating the atomic structure of matter by analysing the diffraction of $X$ radiation as it passes through the substance
being investigated is called \redem{$X$-ray structure analysis}. This method proves most efficient in studying the
structure of crystalline bodies. The diffraction pattern,
registered on photographic film, is called the $X$-ray photograph, or pattern, of the specimen.
\zhpar{通过分析 $X$ 射线穿过被研究物质时的衍射来研究物质原子结构的实验方法，称为\redem{$X$ 射线结构分析}。该方法在研究晶体物质结构方面最为有效。记录在照相底片上的衍射图样，称为样品的 $X$ 射线照片（或图样）。}

An $X$-ray photograph, obtained in the diffraction of a beam of ``white'' $X$-radiation (with a continuous frequency spectrum) by a \redem{monocrystal} is called a \redem{Laue diffraction pattern}. It consists of discrete diffraction (Laue pattern) spots whose position is determined by the Laue conditions \ssect{33.4.1}. A \redem{Debye-Scherrer-Hull powder photograph}, or \redem{X-ray powder photograph}, is an $X$-ray photograph obtained in the diffraction of a beam of monochromatic $X$-radiation by a \redem{polycrystalline} specimen (for instance, a crystalline powder).
Such a photographic pattern consists of a system of concentric
diffraction rings. The radii of the rings $r = l \tan 2\theta$, where $l$ is the distance from the specimen to the plane of the $X$-ray powder photograph, perpendicular to the incident ray, and angles $\theta$ comply with the Wulff-Bragg condition \ssect{33.4.4}.
\zhpar{用“白色”$X$ 射线（具有连续频谱）照射\redem{单晶}所得到的 $X$ 射线照片，称为\redem{劳厄衍射图样}。它由分立的衍射斑点（劳厄斑）组成，斑点的位置由劳厄条件（\ssect{33.4.1}）确定。\redem{德拜-谢乐-霍尔粉末照片}，又称\redem{$X$ 射线粉末照片}，是用单色 $X$ 射线束照射\redem{多晶}样品（例如结晶粉末）所得到的 $X$ 射线照片。这种照片由一系列同心衍射环组成。环的半径为 $r = l \tan 2\theta$，其中 $l$ 为样品到与入射光线垂直的 $X$ 射线粉末照片平面的距离，角 $\theta$ 满足乌尔夫-布拉格条件（\ssect{33.4.4}）。}

\section{Resolving Power of Optical Instruments}
\label{sec-33.5} 
\zhtitle{光学仪器的分辨本领}

\subsection{}\label{33.5.1} The image of the object in any optical system (telescope, microscope, photographic camera, etc.) is obtained by means of a restricted beam of light admitted into the instrument by the so-called \redem{aperture diaphragm}. Such a diaphragm may be that of a photographic camera, lens mount of a telescope, etc. Reducing the diameter of the aperture diaphragm helps to decrease various distortions of the image resulting from the use of wide beams
of light and called \redem{geometrical aberrations of the optical system}.
\zhpar{光学系统（望远镜、显微镜、照相机等）中物体的像，是通过由所谓\redem{孔径光阑}允许进入仪器的受限光束获得的。这种光阑可以是照相机的光圈、望远镜物镜的镜框等。减小孔径光阑的直径，有助于减小由宽光束所引起的各种像的畸变，这种畸变称为光学系统的\redem{几何像差}。}

But, owing to diffraction of light in an optical instrument, the image of a bright point is not a point, but a bright spot surrounded by a system of concentric interference rings (alternating dark and bright ones in the case of monochromatic light and iridescent in the case of white light). This phenomenon limits the \redem{resolving power (resolution) of the optical instrument}, i.e. its capacity to provide separate images of two points of the object close to each other.
\zhpar{但是，由于光在光学仪器中的衍射，一个亮点的像并不是一个点，而是一个亮斑，周围环绕着一组同心干涉环（单色光时为明暗相间的环，白光时为彩色的环）。这一现象限制了光学仪器的\redem{分辨本领（分辨率）}，即其分别给出物体上两个彼此靠近的点的单独像的能力。}

\subsection{}\label{33.5.2} According to \redem{Rayleigh's criterion}, the images of two identical point sources of light can still be seen separately if the central maximum of the diffraction pattern of one falls exactly on the first minimum of the diffraction pattern of the other. It follows from \ssect{33.3.3} that in accordance with the Rayleigh
criterion, two close stars, observed in a telescope with monochromatic light of the wavelength $\lambda$, can be resolved (viewed separately) if the angular separation (distance) between them is 
\zhpar{根据\redem{瑞利判据}，若一个点光源的衍射图样的中央极大恰好落在另一个相同点光源衍射图样的第一极小上，则两个相同点光源的像仍可以被分别看到。由\ssect{33.3.3}可知，依据瑞利判据，用波长为 $\lambda$ 的单色光在望远镜中观察时，两颗靠近的恒星只有在它们的角间距（距离）满足下列条件时才能被分辨（分别看到）：}
\begin{equation*}%
\Delta \varphi \geqslant 1.22 \frac{\lambda}{D},
\end{equation*}
where $D$ is the diameter of the objective. The quantity $(\Delta \varphi)_{0} = = 1.22 \lambda/D$ is called the \redem{angular resolution limit of the telescope}. The reciprocal quantity $1/(\Delta \varphi)_{0}$ is called the resolving power of the telescope. The resolving power of a telescope increases in proportion to the diameter of its objective. The condition for the resolution of a terrestrial telescope and a photographic camera in viewing and photographing distant items coincides with
that for an astronomical telescope.
\zhpar{式中 $D$ 为物镜的直径。量 $(\Delta \varphi)_{0} = 1.22 \lambda/D$ 称为\redem{望远镜的角分辨极限}。其倒数 $1/(\Delta \varphi)_{0}$ 称为望远镜的分辨本领。望远镜的分辨本领与其物镜直径成正比。地面望远镜和照相机在观察与拍摄远处物体时的分辨条件，与天文望远镜的分辨条件相同。}

The \redem{angular resolution limit of the eye} is determined by the diffraction of light by the pupil ($D \approx \SI{2}{\milli\meter}$) and the granular structure of the retina. This limit is about one minute of arc.
\zhpar{\redem{人眼的角分辨极限}由瞳孔（$D \approx \SI{2}{\milli\meter}$）的光衍射以及视网膜的颗粒结构决定。该极限约为一角分。}

\subsection{}\label{33.5.3} The \redem{resolving power of a microscope} is characterized bythe quantity (AZ)0, the shortest distance between two points of the object that are separately visible on the image. In the case of a self-luminous object, all of whose points can be assumed to be incoherent sources, 
\zhpar{\redem{显微镜的分辨本领}由量 $(\Delta l)_{0}$ 表征，它是物体上两点之间能在像中被分别看到的最短距离。对于自发光物体，其所有点都可以看作非相干光源，此时}
\begin{equation*}%
(\Delta l)_{0} = \frac{0.61 \lambda_{0}}{A},
\end{equation*}
where $\lambda_{0}$ is the wavelength of the light in vacuum, $A = n \sin u$ is the \redem{numerical aperture of the objective}, $n$ is the refractive index of the medium between the object and the objective, and $u$ is one half of the aperture angle of the beam of light issuing from a point of the object and entering the microscope objective (i.e. the angle between the outermost ray entering the objective and the optical axis). For non-self-luminous objects, the value of $(\Delta l)_{0}$ depends upon the illumination conditions. But in this case as well, $(\Delta l)_{0} \geqslant \lambda_{0}/A$.
\zhpar{式中 $\lambda_{0}$ 为真空中的光波长，$A = n \sin u$ 为物镜的\redem{数值孔径}，$n$ 为物体与物镜之间介质的折射率，$u$ 为从物体上一点发出并进入显微物镜的光束孔径角之半（即进入物镜的最边缘光线与光轴之间的夹角）。对于非自发光物体，$(\Delta l)_{0}$ 的值取决于照明条件。但即便在这种情况下，也有 $(\Delta l)_{0} \geqslant \lambda_{0}/A$。}

The resolving power of a microscope can be improved either
by reducing the wavelength $\lambda_{0}$, or by increasing the numerical aperture $A$ (numerical aperture is abbreviated N.A.). The former method is employed in ultraviolet and electron microscopy, and the latter, in the immersion microscope, in which the space between the objective and the object is filled with a transparent liquid having a refractive index $n > 1$.
\zhpar{提高显微镜分辨本领的方法有两种：减小波长 $\lambda_{0}$，或增大数值孔径 $A$（数值孔径简写为 N.A.）。前一种方法用于紫外显微术和电子显微术，后一种方法用于浸液显微镜，即在物镜与物体之间充满折射率 $n > 1$ 的透明液体。}

\section{Holography}
\label{sec-33.6} 
\zhtitle{全息术}


\subsection{}\label{33.6.1} \redem{Holography} is a technique for obtaining a three-dimensional optical image of objects and is based on the phenomenon of wave interference. In contrast to the conventional photographic method, light-sensitive fine-grained photographic emulsion is used to record the relations, not only between the amplitudes (or their squares, i.e. the intensities) of light waves scattered by various small portions of the object's surface, but also between
the phases of these waves.
\zhpar{\redem{全息术}是一种获得物体三维光学像的技术，其基础是光的干涉现象。与传统照相方法不同，它使用细粒度的感光乳胶来记录由物体表面各小部分散射的光波振幅（或其平方，即光强）之间的关系，同时也记录这些波的相位之间的关系。}


\begin{figure}%[-3cm]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.9\textwidth]{figs/ch-33/fig-33-10.pdf}
\sidecaption{The principle of holographic imaging.\zhcap{全息成像的原理。}\label{fig-33-10}}
\end{figure}

The essence of the holographic method is made clear in
\figr{fig-33-10}. Photographic plate $P$ (\figr{fig-33-10}~{(a)}) records the interference pattern obtained in superposing wave $\mathit{1}$, scattered by the object $Q$ and called the \redem{signal wave}, or \redem{object beam} and wave $\mathit{2}$, coherent with wave $\mathit{1}$ and having fixed values of amplitude and phase. Wave $\mathit{2}$, called the \redem{reference wave}, or \redem{reference beam}, is emitted by the same source of light that illuminates the object and, after reflection from mirror $B$, falls directly on the
photographic plate $P$. The interference pattern recorded on the photographic plate after it is developed is called the hologram of object $Q$. It consists of a very fine and intricate pattern of alternating interference maxima and minima in the blackening of the photographic emulsion and, in contrast to a photographic image, has no outward resemblance to the object.
\zhpar{全息方法的本质如\figr{fig-33-10}所示。照相底板 $P$（见\figr{fig-33-10}~{(a)}）记录了两列波叠加产生的干涉图样：一列是经物体 $Q$ 散射的波 $\mathit{1}$，称为\redem{物光波}，或\redem{物光束}；另一列是与波 $\mathit{1}$ 相干、且振幅和相位取值固定的波 $\mathit{2}$。波 $\mathit{2}$ 称为\redem{参考光波}，或\redem{参考光束}，它与照明物体的光源来自同一光源，经反射镜 $B$ 反射后直接射到照相底板 $P$ 上。显影后的照相底板上记录的干涉图样，称为物体 $Q$ 的全息图。全息图由极为精细而复杂的、在感光乳胶黑度上交替出现的干涉极大和极小组成，与普通照片不同，它与物体没有任何外观上的相似之处。}

Holograms are obtained by the interference of light at large path differences, i.e. they require an extremely high degree of coher-ence of the light \ssect{32.2.4}. For this reason lasers \ssect{39.8.8} are used in holography as the light source.
\zhpar{全息图是由光在很大光程差下的干涉得到的，因此需要光具有极高的相干性（\ssect{32.2.4}）。为此，全息术采用\redem{激光器}（\ssect{39.8.8}）作为光源。}

\subsection{}\label{33.6.2} \redem{Reconstructing the image of the object} from its hologram $C$ (\figr{fig-33-10}~{(b)}) is accomplished by passing reference wave  $\mathit{2}$, from the same laser used to make the hologram, through it as in the projection of a slide (diapositive). In this operation, the
orientation of the plate with the hologram with respect to the reference wave should also be maintained. Wave  $\mathit{2}$ is diffracted by the hologram. As a result of this diffraction, two three-dimensional images of the object, a virtual and a real image, are seen. The virtual image $Q'$ is at the same place, with respect to the hologram, where the object $Q$ was in taking the photograph. This image is visible in looking through the hologram as if it was a window. The real image $Q"$ is on the other side of the hologram. It seems to he in the air in front of the hologram and
is a mirror image of the object.
\zhpar{利用全息图 $C$（见\figr{fig-33-10}~{(b)}）再现物体的像，其方法是：用与制作全息图所用的激光器相同的光源发出的参考光波 $\mathit{2}$，像投影幻灯片那样使其透过全息图。在这一操作中，全息图板相对于参考光波的取向也必须保持不变。波 $\mathit{2}$ 经全息图衍射后，可以看到物体的两个三维像：一个是虚像，另一个是实像。虚像 $Q'$ 相对于全息图所处的位置，与拍摄照片时物体 $Q$ 所在的位置相同。通过全息图观看时，该像犹如透过窗户所见。实像 $Q"$ 位于全息图的另一侧，它似乎悬浮在全息图前方的空中，并且是物体的镜像。}

Ordinarily, the virtual image of the object is used; in visual
perception it is identical to the object itself. It not only has the property of being three-dimensional, but its perspective varies with the position of the eye of the observer with respect to the hologram. For example, by moving the head along the hologram, it is possible to see behind some item located in the foreground of the holographic image.
\zhpar{通常使用物体的虚像；在目视观察时，它与物体本身完全相同。这种像不仅具有三维特性，而且其透视效果随观察者的眼睛相对于全息图的位置而变化。例如，沿着全息图移动头部，就可以看到全息像前景中某个物体后面的景物。}


\subsection{}\label{33.6.3} The \redem{interference pattern} at each point of the hologram is determined by light scattered by all the points of the object. Hence, any portion of the hologram contains information about the whole object and can enable the image of the whole object to be restored if, in damaging the hologram, only this single portion remains intact. The smaller the size of the saved portion of the damaged hologram, the less the light diffracted by it in restoring the image. The luminance is correspondingly reduced and the sharpness of the holographic image of the object is impaired. Thus, a hologram has essential advantages with respect to reliability of storage of information over a conventional photograph or photographic negative, each element of which contains information only about the portion of the object depicted on it.
\zhpar{全息图上每一点的\redem{干涉图样}都由物体上所有点的散射光决定。因此，全息图的任一部分都包含有关整个物体的信息，即使在全息图受损时只剩下这一小部分完好，也能够还原出整个物体的像。被保留部分越小，衍射后用于再现像的光就越少，像的亮度相应降低，全息像的清晰度也会受到影响。因此，与普通照片或底片相比，全息图在信息存储的可靠性方面具有明显优势，因为后者的每个元素只包含所描绘的那部分物体的信息。}

Holographic recording of information is distinguished for its
high capacity and compact form. A great many holograms can
be recorded on a single photographic plate. For instance, it is sufficient, for this purpose, to record each hologram with
a different value of the angle of incidence of the reference wave on the photographic plate.
\zhpar{全息记录的信息容量大，形式紧凑。在同一张照相底板上可以记录大量全息图，例如，只需以不同的参考光入射角分别记录每一张全息图即可。}

\subsection{}\label{33.6.4} Holography can be used to obtain coloured three-dimensional images of objects. To make such a ``coloured'' hologram, use is made of the monochromatic light of three lasers with three primary colours (for instance, red, green and blue). The recording of the interference patterns, corresponding to light of three wavelengths, is accomplished simultaneously or consecutively on the same photographic plate. To reconstruct the coloured three-dimensional image of the object it is necessary to simultaneously direct onto the hologram the three reference beams of monochromatic light that were used in recording.
\zhpar{利用全息术可以获得物体的彩色三维像。为制作这种“彩色”全息图，需使用三种基色（例如红、绿、蓝）的三台激光器发出的单色光。三种波长对应的干涉图样可以同时或先后记录在同一张照相底板上。为了再现物体的彩色三维像，需要同时将记录时所用的三束单色参考光束照射到全息图上。}

\subsection{}\label{33.6.5} \redem{Three-dimensional holograms} have special properties not possessed by the ordinary kind. They are obtained by using thick-layer photographic emulsions. Recorded on a three-dimensional hologram is a spatial interference pattern, instead
of a plane one. This pattern is obtained in superposing the signal and reference waves. Such a hologram is similar to a spatial diffraction grating (lattice). It is capable of separating out of the incident white light, monochromatic light of the wavelength that was used in recording the hologram. Therefore, an image recorded as a three-dimensional hologram can be reconstructed by illuminating the hologram either by the corresponding monochromatic light or by white light. If a three-dimensional hologram is ``coloured'', the coloured three-dimensional image can be reconstructed by simply illuminating the hologram with white light.
\zhpar{\redem{三维全息图}具有普通全息图所没有的特殊性质。它是利用厚层感光乳胶制作的。三维全息图上记录的是空间干涉图样，而非平面干涉图样。该图样由信号光与参考光叠加得到。这种全息图类似于空间衍射光栅（点阵），能够从入射白光中分离出记录该全息图时所用波长的单色光。因此，记录为三维全息图的像，既可以用相应的单色光照明全息图来再现，也可以用白光照明来再现。若三维全息图是“彩色”的，则只需用白光照明即可再现彩色三维像。}
