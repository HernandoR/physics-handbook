\chapter[Structure Of Solids]{Structure and Certain Properties of Solids}
\label{ch-40}
\zhtitle{固体的结构与某些性质}

\section{Structure of Solids}
\label{sec-40.1} 
\zhtitle{固体的结构}

\subsection{}\label{40.1.1} \redem{Solids} are bodies having constant shape and volume. Distinction is made between \redem{crystalline} and \redem{amorphous} solids.
\zhpar{\redem{固体}是具有一定形状和体积的物体。固体分为\redem{晶体}和\redem{非晶体}两类。}

Externally, crystals have a regular geometric shape. They have
a periodically repeated arrangement of their constituent particles throughout the whole crystal. This arrangement is called the \redem{crystal}, or \redem{space, lattice}. In this sense, a crystal is said to have long-range order (cf. \ssect{13.1.2}). Crystals are bounded by flat faces, orderly arranged with respect to one another, which converge at the \redem{edges} and \redem{vertices}. Large separate crystals having the shape of regular polyhedrons are called \redem{monocrystals}. Their shape is determined by their chemical composition. \redem{Polycrystals} have a fine crystalline structure made up of a great number of small randomly arranged crystals (\redem{crystal grains} or \redem{crystallites}) that have grown together.
\zhpar{从外观上看，晶体具有规则的几何外形。组成晶体的粒子在整个晶体中呈周期性的重复排列，这种排列称为\redem{晶格}，或称\redem{空间点阵}。在这个意义上，晶体具有长程有序（参见\ssect{13.1.2}）。晶体由有规则排列的平面所围成，这些平面相交于\redem{棱}和\redem{顶点}。具有规则多面体形状的较大单个晶体称为\redem{单晶体}，其形状由化学成分决定。\redem{多晶体}具有细小的晶体结构，由大量随机排列、彼此长在一起的小晶体（\redem{晶粒}或\redem{微晶}）组成。}

Amorphous solids (pitch, glass, etc.) are supercooled liquids and have no clearly defined properties of crystals.
\zhpar{非晶体（如沥青、玻璃等）是过冷液体，不具有晶体所特有的明确性质。}

In the following, discussion is confined to crystalline bodies.
\zhpar{以下讨论仅限于晶体。}

\subsection{}\label{40.1.2} Each particle in a crystal lattice is subject to the forces of intermolecular interaction \ssect{12.1.4}. An equilibrium arrangement of all the particles of a solid at the points of.the crystal lattice
\ssect{8.1.4} corresponds to minimum free energy of the crystal \ssect{11.4.5} and its most stable state. The particles at the points of the crystal lattice are located at certain equilibrium distances from one another called the lattice constants.
\zhpar{晶格中的每个粒子都受到分子间相互作用力的作用（参见\ssect{12.1.4}）。固体中所有粒子在晶格点上的平衡排列（参见\ssect{8.1.4}）对应于晶体自由能的最小值（参见\ssect{11.4.5}）以及最稳定的状态。晶格点上的粒子彼此之间处于一定的平衡距离上，这些距离称为晶格常数。}

\subsection{}\label{40.1.3} The main types of crystalline solids, distinguished from one another by the nature of the forces of interaction between their particles and the kinds of particles located at the crystal lattice points, are the following.
\zhpar{晶体的主要类型按照粒子之间相互作用力的性质以及位于晶格点上的粒子种类加以区分，有以下几种。}
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
\item \redem{Ionic crystals} (\ce{NaCl}, calcium carbonate and other salts). Located at the lattice points are regularly alternating positive and negative ions, between which are heteropolar bonds \ssect{39.4.4}.
\zhpar{\redem{离子晶体}（\ce{NaCl}、碳酸钙及其他盐类）。晶格点上规则交替排列着正离子和负离子，它们之间为异极键（参见\ssect{39.4.4}）。}
\item \redem{Valence (covalent) crystals} (\ce{G}, \ce{Ge}, \ce{Te}, etc.). The neutral atoms at the lattice points have homopolar bonds \ssect{39.4.5} between them. Such crystals are found in  semiconductors \ssect{41.10.1} and in many organic solids.
\zhpar{\redem{共价晶体}（\ce{G}、\ce{Ge}、\ce{Te}等）。晶格点上的中性原子之间为同极键（即共价键，参见\ssect{39.4.5}）。这类晶体存在于半导体（参见\ssect{41.10.1}）以及许多有机固体中。}
\item \redem{Molecular crystals} (\ce{Ar}, \ce{CH4}, paraffin, etc.). The points of the crystal lattice are occupied by molecules that are bound by Van der Waals forces, mainly of the dispersion type \ssect{12.1.8}.
\zhpar{\redem{分子晶体}（\ce{Ar}、\ce{CH4}、石蜡等）。晶格点上是分子，它们依靠范德瓦耳斯力相互结合，其中主要是色散型的范德瓦耳斯力（参见\ssect{12.1.8}）。}
\item \redem{Metals} (\ce{Na}, \ce{Cu}, \ce{Al}, etc.). At the lattice points arc positive ions that are formed after the outer (valence) electrons \ssect{39.3.9} have been stripped from the atoms. These electrons form an electron gas \ssect{16.4.1} of collectivized free particles.
\zhpar{\redem{金属}（\ce{Na}、\ce{Cu}、\ce{Al}等）。晶格点上是正离子，它们是原子失去外层（价）电子后形成的（参见\ssect{39.3.9}）。这些电子构成由共有化自由粒子组成的电子气（参见\ssect{16.4.1}）。}
\end{enumerate}

The metallic bond is a specific type of chemical bond \ssect{39.4.1} that is developed between the ions in the crystal lattice and the electron gas. The electrons ``constrict'' the positive ions (mainly by electrostatic forces) and thereby compensate for repulsion between the ions. At distances between the ions equal to the lattice constant \ssect{40.1.2}, the metallic crystal acquires a stable state.
\zhpar{金属键是晶格中的离子与电子气之间形成的一种特殊类型的化学键（参见\ssect{39.4.1}）。电子主要通过静电力“压缩”正离子，从而补偿离子之间的排斥作用。当离子之间的距离等于晶格常数（参见\ssect{40.1.2}）时，金属晶体达到稳定状态。}

\subsection{}\label{40.1.4} The anharmonic nature of the thermal vibration of the particles at the crystal lattice points \ssect{8.1.4} is manifested in the fact that the dependence of the potential energy of interaction of the
particles on their displacement from the equilibrium position is not a parabolic one. The force acting on a particle is not quasi-elastic \ssect{40.3.5}. These facts are of prime importance in understanding certain thermal \ssect{40.2.4} and electrical \ssect{41.5.4} properties of solids.
\zhpar{晶格点上粒子热振动的非简谐性（参见\ssect{8.1.4}）表现为：粒子相互作用势能随其偏离平衡位置的位移的变化关系不是抛物线形的。作用在粒子上的力不是准弹性力（参见\ssect{40.3.5}）。这些事实对于理解固体的某些热学性质（参见\ssect{40.2.4}）和电学性质（参见\ssect{41.5.4}）至关重要。}

\subsection{}\label{40.1.5} A typical property of monocrystals \ssect{40.1.1} is their anisotropy (\redem{crystalline anisotropy}), which is the dependence of the physical properties of solids (thermal, elastic, electrical and optical properties) on the direction in the crystal.
\zhpar{单晶体（参见\ssect{40.1.1}）的一个典型性质是其各向异性（\redem{晶体的各向异性}），即固体的物理性质（热学、弹性、电学和光学性质）随晶体中方向的不同而变化。}

\section{Thermal Expansion of Solids}
\label{sec-40.2} 
\zhtitle{固体的热膨胀}

\subsection{}\label{40.2.1} A solid is subject to \redem{thermal expansion}, of either \redem{linear} or \redem{volume} type, when its temperature is raised. Both kinds of thermal expansion are characterized by the average \redem{coefficients of linear} $\alpha_{l}$ and \redem{cubic}, or \redem{volume}, $\alpha_{V}$ expansion for the given temperature range.
\zhpar{固体在温度升高时会发生\redem{热膨胀}，可分为\redem{线膨胀}和\redem{体膨胀}两类。两类热膨胀都由给定温度范围内的平均\redem{线膨胀系数}$\alpha_{l}$ 和\redem{体膨胀系数}$\alpha_{V}$ 来表征。}

\subsection{}\label{40.2.2} If $l_{0}$ is the length of a body at the temperature \SI{0}{\degreeCelsius}, its elongation $\Delta l$ upon being heated to the temperature t $t \, \si{\degreeCelsius}$ is
\zhpar{若 $l_{0}$ 为物体在 \SI{0}{\degreeCelsius} 时的长度，则当其被加热到 $t \, \si{\degreeCelsius}$ 时，其伸长量 $\Delta l$ 如下式所示：}
\begin{equation*}%
\Delta l = \alpha_{l} l_{0} t,
\end{equation*}
from which $\alpha_{l} = \Delta l/l_{0} t$.
\zhpar{由此得 $\alpha_{l} = \Delta l/l_{0} t$。}

The linear expansion coefficient characterizes the elongation per unit length $\Delta l/l_{0}$ as the body is heated one degree. For most solids $\alpha_{l} \approx  \nu{d-6} \text{ to } \SI{d-5}{\per\kelvin}$ and depends only slightly on the temperature.
\zhpar{线膨胀系数表示物体温度每升高一度时单位长度的伸长量 $\Delta l/l_{0}$。对于大多数固体，$\alpha_{l} \approx \nu{d-6} \text{ to } \SI{d-5}{\per\kelvin}$，并且它只随温度发生微小的变化。}

\subsection{}\label{40.2.3} When a body is heated from \SI{0}{\degreeCelsius} to $t \, \si{\degreeCelsius}$, its volume
increases from $V_{0}$ to $V$ according to the law
\zhpar{当物体从 \SI{0}{\degreeCelsius} 加热到 $t \, \si{\degreeCelsius}$ 时，其体积由 $V_{0}$ 增大到 $V$，服从如下规律：}
\begin{equation*}%
V = V_{0} (1 + \alpha_{V}t),
\end{equation*}
from which $\alpha_{V} = \Delta V/V_{0}t$. The cubic expansion coefficient characterizes the volume increase per unit volume $\Delta V/V_{0}$ as the body is heated by one degree.
\zhpar{由此得 $\alpha_{V} = \Delta V/V_{0}t$。体膨胀系数表示物体温度每升高一度时单位体积的增量 $\Delta V/V_{0}$。}

In a first approximation, the relation between the coefficients $\alpha_{V}$ and $\alpha_{l}$ is of the form
\zhpar{在第一级近似下，系数 $\alpha_{V}$ 与 $\alpha_{l}$ 之间的关系为：}
\begin{equation*}%
\alpha_{V} \approx 3\alpha_{l}.
\end{equation*}

\subsection{}\label{40.2.4} The thermal expansion of solids is due the anharmonicil y of the thermal vibration of the particles in me lattice \ssect{40.1.4}. If $r_{0}$ is the equilibrium distance between adjacent particles, then, at some arbitrary instant, the distance between them is $r = r_{0} +
 x$, where $x$ is the mutual displacement of the particles from their equilibrium positions due to thermal vibration. The forces between the particles in the lattice are not quasi-elastic, but depend on the displacement $x$ according to the relation
\zhpar{固体的热膨胀是由晶格中粒子热振动的非简谐性引起的（参见\ssect{40.1.4}）。设 $r_{0}$ 为相邻粒子之间的平衡距离，则在任一时刻它们之间的距离为 $r = r_{0} + x$，式中 $x$ 为粒子由于热振动而相对平衡位置产生的相互位移。晶格中粒子之间的力不是准弹性的，而是按如下关系随位移 $x$ 变化：}
\begin{equation*}% 
F_{x} = -\varkappa x + bx^{2},
\end{equation*}
where $\varkappa$ is the coefficient of quasi-elastic force \ssect{40.3.5}, and $b$ is the \redem{coefficient of anharmonicity} of the vibration. The term $bx^{2}$ characterizes the deviation of the vibration from its harmonic type due to the different dependences of the forces of attraction and repulsion on the distance.
\zhpar{式中 $\varkappa$ 为准弹性力系数（参见\ssect{40.3.5}），$b$ 为振动的\redem{非简谐系数}。项 $bx^{2}$ 表征由于吸引力与排斥力随距离的变化规律不同而使振动偏离简谐型的程度。}

When a solid is in an equilibrium state the positions of the crystal lattice points should not vary with time and the average force acting on each particle should equal zero:
\zhpar{当固体处于平衡状态时，晶格点的位置不应随时间变化，并且作用在每个粒子上的平均力应等于零：}
\begin{equation*}% 
\expval{F_{x}} = 0.
\end{equation*}


If the vibration of the particles was strictly harmonic $(F_{x} = - \varkappa x)$, the average displacement of the particles would be $\expval{x} = - \expval{F_{x}}/\varkappa = 0$, i.e. there would be no thermal expansion. For real anharmonic vibration, it follows from the condition that
$\expval{F_{x}} = 0$, that
\zhpar{若粒子的振动严格为简谐的（$F_{x} = - \varkappa x$），则粒子的平均位移为 $\expval{x} = - \expval{F_{x}}/\varkappa = 0$，即不存在热膨胀。对于实际的非简谐振动，由条件 $\expval{F_{x}} = 0$ 可得如下关系：}
\begin{equation*}% 
\varkappa \expval{x} + b \expval{x^{2}} = 0 \qor \expval{x} = \frac{b}{\varkappa} \expval{x^{2}}
\end{equation*}
For thermal vibration with small amplitudes the potential energy $E_{p}$ of the vibrating particles is equal \ssect{28.2.3} to:
\zhpar{对于小振幅热振动，振动粒子的势能 $E_{p}$ 等于（参见\ssect{28.2.3}）：}
\begin{equation*}% 
E_{p} = \frac{\varkappa x^{2}}{2}.
\end{equation*}
But, according to the law of equipartition of energy \ssect{10.6.4}, $E_{p} = kT/2$, where $k$ is Boltzmann's constant \ssect{8.4.5} and $T$ is the absolute temperature. Thus
\zhpar{但是，根据能量均分定理（参见\ssect{10.6.4}），$E_{p} = kT/2$，其中 $k$ 为玻尔兹曼常量（参见\ssect{8.4.5}），$T$ 为绝对温度。由此得：}
\begin{align*}% 
\frac{\varkappa \expval{x^{2}}}{2}  & = \frac{kT}{2} \qor \\\expval{x^{2}} & = \frac{kT}{\varkappa} \qor \\
\expval{x} & = \frac{bkT}{\varkappa^{2}}.
\end{align*}
Hence, the average distance between the particles of a solid increases when it is heated, leading to thermal expansion.
\zhpar{因此，固体中粒子之间的平均距离随加热而增大，从而导致热膨胀。}

\section{Brief Information on the Elastic Properties of Solids}
\label{sec-40.3} 
\zhtitle{固体弹性性质简介}


\subsection{}\label{40.3.1} \redem{Deformation} of a solid is the change in its dimensions and volume. Deformation is usually accompanied by a change in the shape of the body. Sometimes (in triaxial tension or compression) no change in shape occurs. Deformation may be caused by external forces acting on the body, a change in its temperature \ssect{40.2.1}
and other reasons.
\zhpar{固体的\redem{形变}是指其尺寸和体积的改变。形变通常伴随着物体形状的改变，但有时（如三向拉伸或压缩）形状并不改变。形变可能由作用在物体上的外力、温度变化（参见\ssect{40.2.1}）以及其他原因引起。}

Deformation of a body leads to displacement of its particles from their initial equilibrium positions at the crystal lattice points \ssect{8.1.1} to new positions. This displacement is opposed by the forces of interaction between the particles. Internal elastic forces are developed inside the body to counterbalance the external forces causing the deformation.
\zhpar{物体的形变使其粒子从晶格点上的初始平衡位置（参见\ssect{8.1.1}）移动到新的位置。这种位移受到粒子间相互作用力的阻碍。物体内部产生内弹性力，以平衡引起形变的外力。}

\subsection{}\label{40.3.2} Deformation is said to be elastic (\redem{elastic deformation}) if it disappears as soon as the external forces causing it cease to act. At this the particles of the solid return to their initial positions of equilibrium \ssect{40.3.1}. Inelastic deformation of a solid leads to irreversible rearrangement of the crystal lattice \ssect{40.1.1} and the shape of the body is not restored when the external forces are removed. Such deformation is said to be \redem{plastic} \ssect{40.3.8}. The transformation of elastic deformation into plastic deformation may occur upon prolonged action of even small external forces applied to the body. The reverse transformation cannot occur.
\zhpar{若形变在引起形变的外力停止作用后立即消失，则称该形变为弹性的（\redem{弹性形变}）。此时固体的粒子回到其平衡的初始位置（参见\ssect{40.3.1}）。固体的非弹性形变会导致晶格的不可逆重排（参见\ssect{40.1.1}），外力去除后物体的形状不能恢复。这种形变称为\redem{塑性}形变（参见\ssect{40.3.8}）。即使是很小的外力长时间作用于物体，弹性形变也可能转变为塑性形变，但逆向转变不可能发生。}

The \redem{degree of elasticity} is the ratio of the work that can be done by a deformed body when the deforming forces are slowly removed to the work done by external forces in deforming the body.
\zhpar{\redem{弹性度}是指形变物体在形变力被缓慢撤除时所能做的功，与外力使物体发生形变时所做的功之比。}

\subsection{}\label{40.3.3} The physical quantity equal to the elastic force $\dd \vb{F}_{el}$ per unit area $\dd S$ of the cross section of the body being deformed is called the \redem{stress} $\sigma$:
\zhpar{在形变物体的横截面上，单位面积 $\dd S$ 所受的弹性力 $\dd \vb{F}_{el}$ 所对应的物理量称为\redem{应力} $\sigma$，即：}
\begin{equation*}%
\sigma = \dv{\vb{F}_{el}}{S}.
\end{equation*}
The stress is said to be \redem{normal} if force $\dd \vb{F}_{el}$ is normal to the surface $\dd S$ and \redem{tangential}, or \redem{shearing}, if the force is tangential to this surface. 
\zhpar{若力 $\dd \vb{F}_{el}$ 垂直于面 $\dd S$，则应力称为\redem{正应力}；若力与该面相切，则应力称为\redem{切应力}，或称\redem{剪应力}。}

\subsection{}\label{40.3.4} A measure of the deformation is the \redem{unit strain}, or \redem{relative deformation}. It is equal to the ratio of the absolute deformation $\Delta x$ to the initial value of the quantity $x$, which characterizes the shape or dimensions of a deformed body: $\Delta x/x$.
\zhpar{形变的度量是\redem{单位应变}，或称\redem{相对形变}。它等于绝对形变 $\Delta x$ 与表征形变物体形状或尺寸的量 $x$ 的初始值之比，即 $\Delta x/x$。}
\begin{marginfigure}%[-3cm] 
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{figs/ch-40/fig-40-01.pdf}
\sidecaption{Stress as a function of the unit deformation.\zhcap{应力随单位形变的变化。}\label{fig-40-01}}
\end{marginfigure}
\redem{Hooke's law} states that the stress in an elastically deformed body is proportional to its unit strain. Thus
\zhpar{\redem{胡克定律}指出，弹性形变物体中的应力与其单位应变成正比。即：}
\begin{equation*}%
\sigma = K_{x} \frac{\Delta x}{x},
\end{equation*}
where $K_{x}$ is the modulus of elasticity, equal in magnitude to the stress developed in relative deformation equal to unity. The quantity $\alpha_{x} = 1/K_{x}$ is called the \redem{coefficient of elasticity}. Hooke's law is valid only for sufficiently small relative deformation. The stress $\sigma_{p}$ at which the deformation is no longer proportional to the stress is called the \redem{proportional elastic limit} (point $A$ in \figr{fig-40-01}).
\zhpar{式中 $K_{x}$ 为弹性模量，其数值等于相对形变为一时所产生的应力。量 $\alpha_{x} = 1/K_{x}$ 称为\redem{弹性系数}。胡克定律仅在相对形变足够小时才成立。形变不再与应力成正比时对应的应力 $\sigma_{p}$ 称为\redem{比例弹性极限}（见\figr{fig-40-01}中的 $A$ 点）。}




\subsection{}\label{40.3.5} In addition to elastic forces, there are forces of other origin than elastic forces, but which satisfy the equation
\zhpar{除弹性力外，还存在其他来源的力，它们满足下列关系：}
\begin{equation*}%
F_{x} = - \varkappa x,
\end{equation*}
where $F_{x}$ is the projection of the force on the direction in which the absolute linear deformation $x$ takes place. They are called \redem{quasi-elastic forces}. The quantity $x$ is called the \redem{coefficient of the quasi-elastic force}.
\zhpar{式中 $F_{x}$ 为力在绝对线形变 $x$ 发生方向上的投影。这类力称为\redem{准弹性力}。量 $x$ 称为\redem{准弹性力系数}。}

\subsection{}\label{40.3.6} The simplest type of deformation is \redem{longitudinal}, or \redem{uniaxial}, \redem{extension} (or \redem{compression}), which consists in elongating (shortening) a body by the action of the tensile (compressive) force $F$. Elastic extension (compression) ceases when $F = F_{el}$ where $F_{el}$ is the elastic force \ssect{40.3.1}. The unit strain (relative deformation) $\Delta x/x = \Delta l/l$, where $\Delta l$ is the change in length due to the action of force $F$, and $l$ is the initial length of the body.
\zhpar{最简单的形变类型是\redem{纵向}，或\redem{单轴}，\redem{拉伸}（或\redem{压缩}），即在拉（压）力 $F$ 的作用下使物体伸长（缩短）。弹性拉伸（压缩）在 $F = F_{el}$ 时停止，其中 $F_{el}$ 为弹性力（参见\ssect{40.3.1}）。单位应变（相对形变）为 $\Delta x/x = \Delta l/l$，其中 $\Delta l$ 为力 $F$ 作用下的长度改变量，$l$ 为物体的初始长度。}

According to Hooke's law
\zhpar{根据胡克定律，有如下关系：}
\begin{equation*}%
\sigma = \frac{F}{S} = E \frac{\Delta l}{l},
\end{equation*}
where the modulus of elasticity $K_{x} = E$ is called \redem{Young's modulus}. Young's modulus is equal to the normal stress $\sigma$ at which the linear dimension of the body is doubled: $\Delta l = l$.\sidenote{Assuming that Hooke's law is valid for such large deformation.}
\zhpar{式中弹性模量 $K_{x} = E$ 称为\redem{杨氏模量}。杨氏模量在数值上等于使物体线性尺寸加倍（$\Delta l = l$）时的正应力 $\sigma$。}
\zhpar{注：假定胡克定律在如此大的形变下仍然成立。}

\subsection{}\label{40.3.7} The dependence of the
normal stress a on the unit strain $\Delta l/l$ in uniaxial extension is called the \redem{tensile stress-strain diagram}
(\figr{fig-40-01}). Beyond the proportional elastic limit \ssect{40.3.4}, an increase in a leads to a considerable increase in $\Delta l/l$. At the stress $\sigma_{y}$, corresponding to point in the stress-strain diagram, the unit
strain continues to increase without increasing the stress (horizontal portion $BB'$ in the diagram). The stress $\sigma_{y}$ is called the \redem{yield point}. The maximum stress at that the body can withstand before rupture, point $C$ in the diagram, is called the \redem{ultimate strength} or, if the body is in tension, the \redem{tensile strength}. Point $D$ corresponds to the rupture of the body.
\zhpar{正应力 $\sigma$ 与单轴拉伸时单位应变 $\Delta l/l$ 之间的关系称为\redem{拉伸应力-应变图}（见\figr{fig-40-01}）。超过比例弹性极限（参见\ssect{40.3.4}）后，$\sigma$ 的增大会使 $\Delta l/l$ 显著增加。在应力 $\sigma_{y}$ 所对应的点上，单位应变继续增大而应力不再增加（图中水平段 $BB'$）。应力 $\sigma_{y}$ 称为\redem{屈服点}。物体在断裂前所能承受的最大应力（图中 $C$ 点）称为\redem{极限强度}，若物体处于拉伸状态，则称为\redem{抗拉强度}。$D$ 点对应于物体的断裂。}

\subsection{}\label{40.3.8} If the load on a body, deformed to the stress $\sigma_{a}$ (point $a$ in the diagram in \figr{fig-40-01}) is slowly removed, the curve $\sigma =
 \varphi (\Delta l/l)$ is the straight line $aR$, parallel to the straight portion $OA$ of the diagram. The line segment $OR$ determines the \redem{permanent set}, typical of plastic deformation.
\zhpar{若物体在应力 $\sigma_{a}$（图中 $a$ 点）下发生形变后，缓慢撤除载荷，则曲线 $\sigma = \varphi (\Delta l/l)$ 为平行于图中直线段 $OA$ 的直线 $aR$。线段 $OR$ 确定了\redem{永久变形}，它是塑性形变的典型特征。}

\centering
\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{figs/ch-40/fig-40-02.pdf}
\sidecaption{Shear in a body.\zhcap{物体中的剪切。}\label{fig-40-02}}
\end{marginfigure}

\subsection{}\label{40.3.9} \redem{Shear} is the deformation of a body in which all its flat layers, parallel to a certain \redem{shear plane}, are displaced parallel in one another without distortion or any changes in dimensions (\figr{fig-40-02}). Shear results from the action of tangential force $\vb{F}$, applied to face $BC$, parallel to the shear plane. Face $AD$, parallel to face $BC$, is fixed. For small shear deformation
\zhpar{\redem{剪切}是物体的一种形变，其中物体所有平行于某一\redem{剪切面}的平面层彼此平行地发生位移，既无畸变，尺寸也无任何改变（见\figr{fig-40-02}）。剪切是由作用在 $BC$ 面上且平行于剪切面的切向力 $\vb{F}$ 引起的。与 $BC$ 面平行的 $AD$ 面保持固定。对于微小剪切形变，有：}
\begin{equation*}%
\gamma \approx \tan \gamma = \frac{\overline{CC'}}{\overline{CD}},
\end{equation*}
where $\overline{CC'} = \Delta x$ is the absolute shear, $\gamma$ is the \redem{shear angle}, or  \redem{unit shear strain}, expressed in radians. According to Hooke's law \ssect{40.3.4}, the unit shear strain is proportional To the tangential (shearing) stress $\sigma_{\tau} = F/S$, where $S$ is the area of $BC$, i.e.
\zhpar{式中 $\overline{CC'} = \Delta x$ 为绝对剪切量，$\gamma$ 为\redem{切变角}，或称\redem{单位切应变}，以弧度表示。根据胡克定律（参见\ssect{40.3.4}），单位切应变与切向（剪切）应力 $\sigma_{\tau} = F/S$ 成正比，其中 $S$ 为 $BC$ 面的面积，即：}
\begin{equation*}%
\sigma_{\tau} = G \gamma.
\end{equation*}
The quantity $G$ is cafled the \redem{shear modulus}. Its magnitude is equal to the tangential, or shear, stress that produces a unit shear strain equal to unity in the specimen.
\zhpar{量 $G$ 称为\redem{切变模量}。其数值等于在试样中产生等于一的单位切应变时所需的切向（剪切）应力。}

\section{Basic Concepts of Phase Transitions in Solids}
\label{sec-40.4}
\zhtitle{固体中相变的基本概念}

\subsection{}\label{40.4.1} The heating of a solid crystalline body leads to an increase in the amplitude of the anharmonic thermal vibration at the points of the crystal lattice \ssect{40.2.4} and to an increase in the average
interpoint distances in the lattice (thermal expansion, \ssect{40.2.1}). Intense heating destroys the crystal lattice and leads to the transition of the substance from its solid to its liquid or vapour phase (first-order phase transition, \ssect{12.4.2}).
\zhpar{晶体受热时，晶格点上非简谐热振动的振幅增大（参见\ssect{40.2.4}），晶格中粒子的平均间距也随之增大（热膨胀，参见\ssect{40.2.1}）。剧烈加热会破坏晶格，使物质由固态转变为液态或气态（一级相变，参见\ssect{12.4.2}）。}

\subsection{}\label{40.4.2} The transition of a substance from the solid to the gaseous state is called \redem{sublimation}. Transition from the solid to the liquid state is called \redem{melting}, or \redem{fusion}. Melting begins at a definite temperature $T_{m}$ for the given pressure; it is called the melting point. This temperature remains constant during the melting process. 
\zhpar{物质由固态直接转变为气态称为\redem{升华}。由固态转变为液态称为\redem{熔化}，或称\redem{熔解}。在给定压强下，熔化在确定的温度 $T_{m}$ 开始，该温度称为熔点。熔化过程中温度保持不变。}

\begin{marginfigure}%[-3cm] 
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{figs/ch-40/fig-40-03.pdf}
\sidecaption{Dependence of temperature and state.\zhcap{温度与物态的关系。}\label{fig-40-03}}
\end{marginfigure}

Shown in \figr{fig-40-03} is the dependence $T (Q)$, where $Q$ is the quantity of heat \ssect{9.2.1} delivered to the solid being heated. The isothermal portion $BC$ corresponds to a two-phase system: solid-liquid (cf. \ssect{12.3.2}, where the two-phase system consists of a liquid and saturated vapour). In the melting process, the substance is converted from a more ordered crystalline state to a less ordered liquid state. According to the second law of thermodynamics \ssect{11.3.2}, melting is associated with an increase in the entropy \ssect{11.4.3} of the system.
\zhpar{如\figr{fig-40-03}所示，给出的是 $T (Q)$ 的关系，其中 $Q$ 为传给正在被加热的固体的热量（参见\ssect{9.2.1}）。等温段 $BC$ 对应于固-液两相系统（参见\ssect{12.3.2}，其中两相系统由液体和饱和蒸气组成）。在熔化过程中，物质由较有序的晶态转变为较无序的液态。根据热力学第二定律（参见\ssect{11.3.2}），熔化与系统熵的增加有关（参见\ssect{11.4.3}）。}

\subsection{}\label{40.4.3} The quantity of heat required to melt unit mass of a solid at the temperature $T_{m}$ is called the \redem{heat of fusion}, or \redem{of melting}. It is also called the \redem{latent heat}. It follows from the first law of thermodynamics \ssect{9.3.1} that
\zhpar{使单位质量的固体在温度 $T_{m}$ 下熔化所需的热量称为\redem{熔解热}，或称\redem{熔化热}，它也称为\redem{潜热}。由热力学第一定律（参见\ssect{9.3.1}）可得：}
\begin{equation*}%
r_{m} = u_{l} - u_{s} + p (v_{l} - v_{s}),
\end{equation*}
where $u_{l}$ and $u_{s}$ are the specific internal energies \ssect{9.1.2} of the substance (internal energies per unit volume) in the liquid and solid phases, $v_{l}$ and $v_{s}$ are the specific volumes of the liquid and solid phases, and $p$ is the constant pressure at which the first-order transition proceeds \ssect{12.4.2}.
\zhpar{式中 $u_{l}$ 和 $u_{s}$ 分别为物质在液相和固相中的比内能（即单位体积的内能，参见\ssect{9.1.2}），$v_{l}$ 和 $v_{s}$ 分别为液相和固相的比容，$p$ 为一级相变进行时保持不变的压强（参见\ssect{12.4.2}）。}

\subsection{}\label{40.4.4} 
\begin{marginfigure}%[-1.5cm] 
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.7\textwidth]{figs/ch-40/fig-40-04.pdf}
\sidecaption{Dependence of phases on pressure and temperature, a $pT$ diagram.\zhcap{物相随压强和温度的变化，即 $pT$ 图。}\label{fig-40-04}}
\end{marginfigure}

The melting point Tm depends upon the pressure $p$. A change of $\dd p$ in the pressure leads to a change in the melting point by $\dd T_{m}$. The dependence $T_{m} = Tm ( p)$ is expressed by the Clapeyron-Clausius equation (see also \ssect{13.6.5}):
\zhpar{熔点 $T_{m}$ 取决于压强 $p$。压强改变 $\dd p$ 时，熔点改变 $\dd T_{m}$。关系 $T_{m} = Tm ( p)$ 由克拉珀龙-克劳修斯方程给出（另见\ssect{13.6.5}）：}
\begin{equation*}%
\dv{T_{m}}{p} = \frac{T_{m} (v_{l} - v_{s})}{r_{m}}.
\end{equation*}
The notation is defined in \ssect{40.4.3}. The Clapeyron-Clausius (equation is valid for any first-order phase transition. 
\zhpar{此处符号的定义见\ssect{40.4.3}。克拉珀龙-克劳修斯方程适用于任何一级相变。}

In the overwhelming majority of cases the melting of a substance leads to an increase in its specific volume, i.e. $v_{l} > v_{s}$ and ($\dv*{T_{m}}{p} > 0$, $(r_{m} >0)$, i.e. the melting point increases with the pressure. Such substances can be in the solid state at high pressures in the temperature region above the critical temperature \ssect{12.3.1}.
\zhpar{绝大多数物质熔化时比容增大，即 $v_{l} > v_{s}$，且（$\dv*{T_{m}}{p} > 0$，$(r_{m} >0)$），亦即熔点随压强增大而升高。这类物质在高压下，可以在高于临界温度（参见\ssect{12.3.1}）的温度区域内处于固态。}

\begin{marginfigure}%[3cm] 
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.6\textwidth]{figs/ch-40/fig-40-05.pdf}
\sidecaption{Dependence of phases on pressure and temperature, a $pT$ diagram.\zhcap{物相随压强和温度的变化，即 $pT$ 图。}\label{fig-40-05}}
\end{marginfigure}

The specific volume of certain substances (water, bismuth, gallium) increases in melting, so that $v_{l} < v_{s}$. Ice, for instance, at \SI{0}{\degreeCelsius} has a lower density than water. For such substances $\dv*{T_{m}}{p} < 0$, i.e. the melting point decreases as the pressure is increased.
\zhpar{某些物质（如水、铋、镓）在熔化时比容减小，即 $v_{l} < v_{s}$。例如，\SI{0}{\degreeCelsius} 时冰的密度小于水。对于这类物质，$\dv*{T_{m}}{p} < 0$，即熔点随压强增大而降低。}




\subsection{}\label{40.4.5} In a $pT$ diagram, the dependence
of the temperature on the pressure $p = f (T)$ in a first-order
phase transition is represented by a curve on which each point
corresponds to equilibrium of two existing phases. The vapour
generation curve, for example, shows the equilibrium of the
two-phase system: liquid-vapour. Since $v_{v} - v_{l} > 0$ (where $v_{v}$ is the specific volume of the vapour), it follows from the Clapeyron-Clausius equation \ssect{40.4.4}, that $\dv*{p}{T} > 0$. The curve $p = f (T)$, separating the regions of the liquid and gaseous states of a substance, ends at critical point $C$ \ssect{12.3.1} (\figr{fig-40-04}). Shown in \figr{fig-40-05} and \figr{fig-40-06} are the equilibrium
curves of the two-phase solid-liquid states for the cases
when $v_{l} > v_{s}$ and $v_{l} < v_{s}$, respectively.
\zhpar{在 $pT$ 图中，一级相变中压强与温度的关系 $p = f (T)$ 用一条曲线表示，曲线上的每一点对应于两相共存的平衡状态。例如，汽化曲线表示液-气两相系统的平衡。由于 $v_{v} - v_{l} > 0$（其中 $v_{v}$ 为蒸气的比容），由克拉珀龙-克劳修斯方程（参见\ssect{40.4.4}）可知 $\dv*{p}{T} > 0$。分隔物质液态与气态区域的曲线 $p = f (T)$ 终止于临界点 $C$（参见\ssect{12.3.1}，见\figr{fig-40-04}）。如\figr{fig-40-05}和\figr{fig-40-06}所示，分别为 $v_{l} > v_{s}$ 和 $v_{l} < v_{s}$ 两种情况下固-液两相平衡曲线。}

\begin{marginfigure}%[7.75cm] 
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.6\textwidth]{figs/ch-40/fig-40-06.pdf}
\sidecaption{Dependence of phases on pressure and temperature, a $pT$ diagram.\zhcap{物相随压强和温度的变化，即 $pT$ 图。}\label{fig-40-06}}
\end{marginfigure}

\subsection{}\label{40.4.6} In cooling liquids to a certain temperature, called the \redem{freezing point} $T_{f}$ (or \redem{temperature of solidification}), the substance is solidified (crystallized, or frozen). This is the transition from a liquid to a solid crystalline state. In this process a quantity of heat is evolved that is equal to the heat of melting \ssect{40.4.3}. The freezing point is equal to the melting point and depends upon the presence of impurities in the substance. Impurities lower Tf if they do not form mixed crystals with the substance. These are called solid solutions. Sea water, for instance, containing dissolved salts, freezes at a lower temperature than distilled water. This serves as the basis for producing cooling mixtures.
\zhpar{将液体冷却到某一温度，即\redem{凝固点} $T_{f}$（或称\redem{凝固温度}）时，物质发生凝固（结晶或冻结），即由液态转变为晶态。此过程放出的热量等于熔化热（参见\ssect{40.4.3}）。凝固点等于熔点，并且取决于物质中是否存在杂质。若杂质不与该物质形成混合晶体，则会使 $T_{f}$ 降低。这类混合晶体称为固溶体。例如，溶有盐类的海水，其凝固温度低于蒸馏水。这一现象是制造冷却混合物的基础。}

\subsection{}\label{40.4.7} The freezing (crystallization) of liquids is associated with a change in the nature of the thermal motion of their particles. The time the particles remain in a temporary settled state (relaxation time, see \ssect{13.2.4}) is increased. The forces of mutual attraction between the particles change the thermal motion into random thermal vibration about the points of the crystal lattice. The transition of the substance to a more ordered phase is associated with a reduction in the entropy of the system \ssect{11.4.3}.
\zhpar{液体的凝固（结晶）与其粒子热运动性质的变化有关。粒子停留在暂时定居状态中的时间（弛豫时间，参见\ssect{13.2.4}）增加。粒子间的相互吸引力使热运动转变为围绕晶格点的无规热振动。物质向更有序的相转变与系统熵的减少有关（参见\ssect{11.4.3}）。}
